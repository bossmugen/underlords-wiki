# Underlords publication clearance

Public storytelling and archive custody are separate jobs. A biography may carry the human meaning of a restricted-room scene without publishing the private attachment or exposing the entire source exchange.

## Clearance levels

| Level | Use |
|---|---|
| `PUBLIC` | Safe and approved for the public site. |
| `MEMBERS` | Visible only to authenticated UL members when that layer exists. |
| `BASEMENT` | Source/provenance detail for authorized archive work; not part of ordinary public reading. |
| `SEALED` | Retained privately and not exposed. |

## Inheritance

A quote, attachment, incident, or relationship detail inherits the source room's default clearance unless the project owner explicitly approves broader publication. A public summary does not automatically make its underlying receipt public.

## Public writing rule

Tell the person-first story upstairs. Put provenance, confidence grades, identity joins, and source-method housekeeping behind **SHOW RECEIPTS** or in **THE BASEMENT**. Surface uncertainty naturally only when it changes the story.
