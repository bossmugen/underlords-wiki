# UL site language and flow

## The building

The public experience begins with people, story, rooms, and memory. The underlying database may remain complicated; its schema is not the navigation.

- **UNDERLORDS** — home
- **STRIPPERS** — people and biographies
- **THE FUCK HAPPENED** — chronology and eras
- **ROOMS** — channels and social spaces
- **WALL OF SHAME/FAME** — visual memory
- **SNOOP** — search
- **OTHER CRIMES** — secondary indexes
- **EPISODES & INCIDENTS** — named stories
- **ENTANGLEMENTS** — relationships
- **THE CLOWN CAR** — social-map view
- **EMPLOYMENT FRAUD** — roles and hierarchy
- **BULLSHIT** — running jokes, rituals, and local lore
- **MOUTH CRIMES** — quotes
- **SPEAK ENGLIAH** — glossary
- **THE BASEMENT** — archive and provenance
- **WE FUCKED UP** — corrections

Original UL room/channel language outranks invented ULese whenever it fits.

## Four voices

1. **Narrator:** mostly clean, person-first, emotionally legible prose. The sentence can be ridiculous; its meaning should not be.
2. **Navigation:** short, stable, legible labels. A rare typo may survive as institutional furniture; the whole menu should not look broken.
3. **Utility microcopy:** occasional fast-finger ruptures or autocorrect betrayal, used lightly.
4. **Member voice:** preserve real wording, timing, fragments, typos, corrections, and speaker differences. Do not polish everybody into the narrator.

## Typo rule

UL members can spell. They type quickly enough for letters, punctuation, words, and entire clauses to arrive out of order, and the room understands anyway.

Use plausible transpositions, adjacent-key slips, dropped or doubled words, sentence collisions, punctuation crashes, and believable autocorrect substitutions. Do not invent phonetic misspellings or a fake illiteracy dialect. **SPEAK ENGLIAH** works because it reads like one adjacent-key slip that nobody bothered repairing.

## Page flow

Every major page should offer a meaningful next door: a person, episode, room, era, Wall object, or related piece of bullshit. Use **SINCE YOU'RE SNOOPING**, **PREVIOUSLY**, **THIS COMES BACK LATER**, **EVERYBODY INVOLVED**, and **ELSEWHERE IN THE HOUSE** to explain why the links belong together.

## Archive silence

Do not convert missing archive coverage into a confirmed social event. Prefer “first spotted,” “last surviving appearance,” or “the surviving archive goes quiet” over unsupported claims that somebody joined, left, or stopped caring on a particular date.
