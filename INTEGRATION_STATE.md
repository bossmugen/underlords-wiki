The Run 37 character handoff restores the missing prehistory to Xephy's six-year return. Gilli answers `We'll miss you in game but you're still a UL family member love`, tells Xephy the room will still be there when there is time to talk, and ends with `dont be a stranger bb`. Mugen adds `keep me posted!`.

The word **`still`** is doing the actual work. Gilli names what is ending — the in-game part — and refuses to promote that into loss of the person. `dont be a stranger` is softer than *stay active*; it leaves room for distance. Mugen's `keep me posted!` gives the goodbye a future tense without inventing a deadline.

The wording supplies name + relationship + shared world rather than barging in with an implicit memory test. Mugen remembers. WIKI now owns the full continuity read: the route was explicitly left open in 2020 and later used. This **does not** prove uninterrupted contact across the gap.
