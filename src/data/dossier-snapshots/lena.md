# Lena

Lena was already saying **Underlords** when she reached the Discord welcome desk. On February 16, 2021, she gave the name **Aelena** and her club, and Anthos sent her toward the server tour. By March 11 she had a picture in **Flowers**. The welcome was not necessarily her first day in UL; it was the moment the Discord door opened onto a club she already claimed.

There is more to Lena than that one picture. Around Whiskey she could turn a tiny creature sitting on its own feet into breaking news for Ren and Souta, give a Tinder pose an instant guilty verdict, or bring a confusing social exchange to the room because she genuinely wanted help answering it. She could be both the judge and the person asking the jury for advice. The MAIN biography gives those habits room to breathe; this dossier keeps the dated Photoshoot trail searchable.
