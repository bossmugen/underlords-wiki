# Acoryuie

Acoryuie could be quiet in the room and still care fiercely about finding the right one. Jas had known them elsewhere and invited them into UL. Later, Acoryuie described the place less in terms of rank or visibility than the small conversations they were grateful to have had.

## The familiar doorway

They were already submitting to the Colors and Mafia Photoshoots in April 2021. So when Mugen greeted them in June with **“welcome back bb”** and **“you know your way uwu,”** the warmth had somewhere to land. It was not a stranger being handed a map. Mugen expected them to remember the place, and Acoryuie had already left little traces of themselves in its contests.

The account had answered to **Cinders** earlier and later gave **Juniper** as its own name. The change is part of their history, even though its mechanics remain unclear.

## The people who made returning worth it

In their later telling, the important part was Jas bringing them in and the feeling that this group fit when others had not. Acoryuie did not pretend to be the loudest person in every room. They remembered the people, including the ones they only spoke to briefly. That is what makes the return matter: they had a reason to find their way back.
