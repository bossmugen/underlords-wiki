# Lan

Lan, also known as **LanWangJi**, is a current active UL Sniper. His file is still thin, but it is no longer empty enough that the wiki has to introduce him only by rank.

The first safely recovered authored checkpoint comes from the Lobby on **September 4, 2022**. A message in the pooled Deleted User account answers an intake request with **`Oooh okii ill do it wait a second!`**. One minute later, the same local speaker supplies **`LanWangJi, Underlords`** among the requested fields. That self-identification matters because the underlying Discord user ID is a known archive hazard used by multiple deleted humans elsewhere; it lets the wiki identify this small sequence as Lan without committing the much larger sin of declaring every message on the pooled account his.

The line itself is tiny but useful. `Oooh okii` is not a résumé trait. It is one clean glimpse of how Lan responds in this moment: informal, upbeat, and willing to do the small thing being asked of him without turning it into a production. Then he actually does it. The archive is too sparse to promote that into `Lan is always easygoing` or any other universal temperament, but it finally gives the biography a voice rather than a title card.

The next message makes the scene more interesting. Mugen sees the supplied name and replies **`Ayeee it's LWJ`**. The intake system is asking for identity; Mugen sounds as though the identity is already familiar. `LWJ` arrives as ready-made shorthand, not a nickname being explained for the first time. That supports a simple conclusion: by this checkpoint, Mugen already recognizes LanWangJi / LWJ. It does **not** tell us when they first met, who introduced them, how close they were, or whether this was Lan's first day in UL.

Mugen then sends the usual house directions — Biodata, the Discord Tour/server menu, and Whiskey as main chat. That routing is shared front-door language, so it stays procedure rather than relationship evidence. The human beat is the mismatch underneath it: the server still needs its fields filled; Mugen already knows who walked in.

## What remains open

The direct archive currently surfaced does not yet give Lan a safe bridge from this 2022 Lobby checkpoint to his present Sniper status. His current role is locked by roster canon, but the appointment date, predecessor roles, recruiter history, and person-specific Sniper work remain unresolved. Later pooled Deleted User messages are not assigned to him unless another local identity join proves them.

The same restraint applies to the name. **LanWangJi** is an alias; the name alone is not enough to manufacture a fandom biography or infer personality from the fictional character it references. Lan is also **not Lana**, and he is not the separate 2020 visitor **`lanbo` / Dan**.

So the responsible current story is short but human: a recognized `LWJ` answers a small request with **`Oooh okii ill do it wait a second!`**, follows through almost immediately, and is greeted by Mugen like somebody already known by name. The archive has recovered the doorway and the present title. The middle is still missing, and the wiki leaves it missing until Lan's own receipts bring it back.
