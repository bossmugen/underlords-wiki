# Eos

Eos was not especially interested in making a grand entrance. The Lobby saw the same account under **EnyoCal** and later **Eos~**; the more revealing introduction came from Eos themself, who named **Gilli** as the reason for joining UL. That is a better beginning to their social story than a guessed admission date.

## Relationships

**Gilli** was the person Eos credited for coming to UL. Gilli also knew when to egg them on: when Eos hesitated over posting more Wall material, Gilli's answer was essentially *do it*.

**Marian / Panda** could spend months accusing Eos of being too vanilla to properly shame anyone, then turn around and insist on having Eos around for a shared game. Their running Wall prosecution went both ways: Marian threatened an hourly campaign over Eos's bag, Eos protested loudly, and later kept track of how long the joke had been going. The same relationship also had songs, teasing, hugs, and an Eos reply that Marian had stolen their heart a long time ago. None of it needs a fake formal title to be memorable.

**Mugen** could catch an exhausted Eos describing themself as a corpse and immediately raise a toast to the *pretty corpses*. Eos came back to the thread later as **“this ded corpse”**. The joke was not a one-line drive-by; they both stayed in it.

Marian's **“my only”** gave the dance card its affectionate swagger. Eos wasn't just a name on someone else's entry; they were also a contestant with their own submissions. The pictures remain uninspected, so the caption does the talking.

## Running Gags

**Too vanilla for Screenshot Court.** Marian's accusation did not stop Eos from producing receipts or getting caught in return.

**Corpse on duty.** Eos was exhausted, Mugs was toasting the pretty corpses, and Eos was still available for the taking. The 2026 reunion's resurrection language has an older cousin here, not a proven continuous chain.

**Hamcito, convicted by committee.** Noether turned HamitteY into *hamcito* in a July 2022 Wall exchange. Eos needed approximately no persuasion to adopt the new name, replying in Spanish that hamcito was not innocent. The room was inventing crimes and Eos was already helping prosecute. The joke works because Eos does not need to own the bit to make it worse; somebody else starts it, Eos catches the rhythm, and suddenly the defendant has acquired another accuser. This is the Wall being ridiculous, not a real allegation.

## Petty Crimes

Eos said they were almost finished with computer science and could not wait to graduate. They also told stories about school fights, including the apparently irresistible spectacle of classmates leaving to watch someone pull hair. During Two Truths, One Lie, **liking mashed potatoes** was the lie.
