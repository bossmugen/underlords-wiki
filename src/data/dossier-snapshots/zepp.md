# Zepp

## Cast dossier

Zepp is **Staff**, a Malaysian dentist, and one of Mugen's friends from before Dragon Raja. The useful distinction is that his public weight does not come from a formal role progression or a giant authored archive. It comes from how consistently other people remember a particular kind of Zepp-shaped conversational presence.

Rooks gives the cleanest early label on August 22, 2020: **`I fucking love Zepp’s quotes`**. Ren answers **`Indeed`** five seconds later. That makes `Zepp's quotes` a shared peer category rather than a one-person review. The complete quote set is not recovered, so the dossier keeps the reputation without manufacturing examples.

Run 80 adds a second peer-memory lane that makes the earlier quote reputation easier to understand. Mugen later jokes about **`ZEPP AND HIS 209485 QUESTIONS A DAY`**. The number is obviously hyperbole; the character value is in the target. Zepp asking questions was memorable enough that Mugen could exaggerate the behavior into a fake daily metric and expect the joke to land.

Tae supplies a different caricature: **`walmart key`** and **`A AUTO BOT OF KEY`**. Zepp and Key remain separate people. The exact trait Tae was matching is unresolved, so the wiki does not choose one. What is safe is the social fact that Tae could use Key as a reference point for Zepp at all. Both men already belong to Mugen's older pre-Raja network, making the comparison part of a familiar peer vocabulary rather than an identity bridge.

## Relationships

**Mugen.** Zepp belongs to Mugen's older online circle from before Dragon Raja. Later continuity is casual rather than ceremonial: on January 2, 2022 Mugen explains being awake with **`Dean zep and key texting`**. She does not stop to explain who Zepp is or why he is in that cluster. The line treats the old network as ordinary current life.

**Ren.** Ren occupies two different Zepp memories. She immediately agrees with Rooks that `Zepp's quotes` are a thing, and she also remembers **teaching Zepp how to fish**. The second detail matters because it is not reputation commentary; it is practical shared-game time. It supports familiarity and ordinary contact without turning Ren into a formal mentor or ranking the friendship against anybody else's.

**Key.** Tae's Key comparisons show that Zepp and Key could be socially placed beside one another in peer humor. The jokes do not resolve whether the resemblance was speech, question volume, temperament, or some older-network behavior the room already understood.

**Rooks.** Rooks is the person who explicitly names the `Zepp's quotes` reputation. That does not by itself establish special closeness, but it does show Zepp's verbal style was memorable enough to be talked about as a recurring product category.
