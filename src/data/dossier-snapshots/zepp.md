# Zepp

Zepp is current **Staff**, and he belongs to Mugen's older online circle from before Dragon Raja and Underlords. That provenance matters because it stops the public wiki from accidentally treating his first recovered Discord mention as the beginning of the relationship. The friendship network is older than the server. The Discord evidence, however, is weirdly indirect: Zepp's surviving character file is mostly other people standing in the smoke after whatever he apparently just did.

The cleanest example arrives on August 22, 2020. Rooks writes **`I fucking love Zepp’s quotes`**. Five seconds later Ren answers **`Indeed`**. That wording is stronger than somebody generically saying Zepp is funny. Rooks names a recognizable product category — *Zepp's quotes* — as if the room already knows there is inventory. Ren's immediate concurrence shows the category lands without explanation.

The archive does **not** preserve the underlying quote set in the recovered window. That absence is important. The wiki can publish the reputation receipt; it cannot manufacture a representative Zepp line just because peers tell us there were memorable ones. Ren is also independently visible elsewhere collecting and praising multiple people's quotes, so his agreement is evidence that Zepp belongs inside a wider house habit of quote preservation, not proof of a uniquely close Zepp/Ren relationship.

Nine days later ordinary biography becomes an ensemble event. Mugen states a then-current fact about Zepp. Ansun says **`zepp hii`**. Rooks stops the room with **`wait Zepp is WHAT`**. A pooled Deleted User throws in a nonsense number, and Mugen follows with **`sometimes 5`**. The exact historical age is kept backstage because it does no useful present-day character work. What matters is the reaction: basic Zepp information is apparently surprising enough to become improvisation within seconds.

Then, before dawn on September 15, Mugen files the most UL complaint in the current Zepp record: **`craving chicken nuggets thanks to zoshaa and zepp`**, followed by **`havent had them for years but`**. Zepp and Zoshaa are offscreen causes; Mugen is the visible consequence. That is all the receipt gives us. It does not show Zepp eating nuggets, posting food, recommending food, sending a photo, or saying a specific thing. The safest and funniest version is the one the archive actually supports: two people somehow resurrected a craving Mugen says had been dormant for years.

A September 18 grouping later places **`#9 - mugen, rich, zepp`** together, but the activity behind the numbers is missing. The trio therefore stays an unresolved lead rather than getting promoted into a team, hierarchy, or friendship unit. The same restraint applies to an August Wall fragment where Anthos says `of zepp`; the missing antecedent means we do not know whether the lost object was a picture, quote, screenshot, or description.

This is one of the dossiers where archive discipline is part of the character writing. Zepp matters socially more than the surviving direct-voice count can currently prove. Rooks loves a category of his lines. Ren instantly agrees. Rooks later gets ambushed by a biographical fact. Mugen blames him and Zoshaa for food-craving necromancy. Those are three real ensemble beats, and they are better than padding the page with `Malaysian dentist` until it starts looking like LinkedIn.

The unresolved technical problem is identity resolution: no stable Discord account ID has yet been safely mapped to Zepp. Until that changes, later-room searches by display name can miss him. The wiki therefore treats the thinness as a source boundary, not as evidence that Zepp stopped participating or lacked a personality. **Reputation-by-aftermath** is what the surviving archive earns right now; nothing more is required to make the page honest and specific.
