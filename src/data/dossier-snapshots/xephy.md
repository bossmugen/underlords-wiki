Xephy is a recurring homecoming with a very recognizable voice. The `uwu~` greetings are impossible to miss, but the practical streak is what makes the old Lobby exchanges interesting. When somebody could not see the channels, Xephy followed up. When Mugs changed bot permissions, Xephy tried the commands instead of trusting the settings screen. Sometimes Xephy was also the person who had pointed somebody toward the wrong door. Extremely cute, occasionally the problem, and willing to help fix it.

## Relationships

**Mugen:** Xephy could ask for help with access, test Mugen's fixes, and later reach out directly after years away. The 2026 contact began before the room's retrieval celebration, so this was not a case of the search party dragging an unwilling ghost out of storage.

**Illien:** On the September 2020 rejoin, Xephy's `HAIII ILLIEN` follows the more vulnerable hope that somebody remembers them. Recognition becomes a greeting rather than a membership hearing.

**Xerenity:** The wrong-server exchange is a good miniature of Xephy's style: a mistake gets an immediate `sowe`, not an elaborate defense.

## Episodes

**May 2020 — The Door and the Bot.** Xephy helps check channel access and later tests whether the bot restriction works. One bot remains available, so the fix needs another pass. There is no technical title attached to this; Xephy just keeps checking.

**September 2020 — The First Surviving Return.** `I rejoined the club` and `I mished everyone` put a prior absence into Xephy's own words. The precise leave date is unknown, but the wish to be remembered is unmistakable.

**August 2026 — Home Before the Search Party.** Xephy contacts Mugen first. QOTD celebrates finding Xephy afterward. The following day, memories of raids, photos and the group chat lead to a simple conclusion: different generations can still recognize one another as UL.

## Running Gags

The `uwu~` voice can accompany both affectionate greetings and surprisingly methodical troubleshooting. Xephy's habit of returning with the same verbal fingerprints is practically its own continuity device.
