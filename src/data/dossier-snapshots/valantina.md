# Valantina

Valantina had already been greeted as **val** at UL's Lobby in May 2020. The August 2021 doorway gives a sharper picture: when asked for her club, she answered **Council**, and Gabu pointed her toward the server's Photoshoot submissions. An outside-club visitor, shown exactly where to join the nonsense.

Eleven hours later she had a POPSTAR image ready and no confidence whatsoever in the clock: **`IDK IF I CAN STILL SUBMIT THIS BUT I WILL ANYWAYS`**. The doubt is the first half of the sentence; pressing send is the second. In September she came back for FESTIVAL without the all-caps preamble. The record supports a repeat participant, not an invented contest career.
