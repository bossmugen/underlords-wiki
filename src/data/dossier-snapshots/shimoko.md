# Shimoko

Ren did not wait for the welcome bot to finish. **“SHI!!!!!!”** and a pile of happy emotes greeted Shimoko before the usual Lobby questions had been settled. Ren's next concern was adulthood. Shimoko would have preferred to opt out — **“Wish to not be adult uwu”** — but confirmed it anyway. Gabu welcomed them, assigned Discord access, and pointed toward the server tour.

The contrast is the whole introduction: Ren made the entrance loud; Shimoko barely needed a speech. Two weeks later, Shimoko dropped an image into the May **RELAX** submissions without a caption.
