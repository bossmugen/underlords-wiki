# Snow

Snow is a current Pit Boss / Minister, holds the Judgemen appointment, and is also a Game Officer. One correction stays explicit: he was **not** Mugen's formal second-in-command; the current Underboss seats belong to Gabu and Anayss. His archive is strongest when it stops trying to rank him and instead follows the kind of problems he repeatedly chose to solve.

## APRIL 2020 — FIX THE DOOR, THEN EXPLAIN WHY PEOPLE SHOULD FEEL SAFE USING THE ROOM

On April 7, Mugen reports that she can no longer assign member roles. Snow says he will investigate while blaming **`discord gnomes`** and proposing the technical toolkit of a wrench, burlap bag, and duct tape. Mugen later thanks him for fixing the problem.

Six days later, when Slice opens, Snow defines the room as somewhere members can share pictures, keep each other updated, and **`get to know each other outside of game`**. In the same opening sequence he warns people not to expose location or personal information and makes photo sharing explicitly optional: **`only if you want`**, **`we dont pressure here`**.

Those scenes belong together chronologically. Snow's early contribution is not simply "technical guy" or "rules guy." He is helping make access work and then helping define the limits around access once people start using it.

## AUGUST 2020 — THE INFORMAL GUARDRAILS BECOME A FORMAL DRAFT

By August, Snow is drafting the JAG / Club Conduct framework. The notorious 24 **Yeeting charges** borrow formal military-law structure while Snow simultaneously says UL is **`in no way a military organization`**.

Under the joke formatting, the draft repeatedly limits what people with access or power can do. Real life takes priority. New players should be treated kindly. Hate speech is bannable. Personal identifying information should be protected. Charge 15 addresses private-area viewing or recording without consent and obscene material sent through DMs without consent. Other charges address Officer abuse of special trust, retaliation against reporters, interference, obstruction, and destruction or concealment of chat records.

The draft was under construction and not every clause is treated as final prosecuted law. The biographical progression is still clear: April's room-level privacy instincts have become an attempt to write boundaries into the institution itself.

## 2021–2023 — KNOWING THE SYSTEM ALSO MEANS ADMITTING WHEN THE SYSTEM HAS MOVED ON WITHOUT YOU

In January 2021, Saber needs attention. Snow says he no longer remembers how to fix it and asks Gabu if she wants to try. Gabu says she can work through it one item at a time. Over the next two years Snow remains visible around event commands, server-time testing, timezone problems, and edits. In March 2023, after the command surface has changed again, he tries `/help` and simply says **`Forgot all the commands 😱`**.

That is a useful correction to the mythology competence can create around technical people. Snow is willing to say **I don't remember** and move the work toward the actual problem. The system matters more than maintaining the performance of infallibility.

## 2020–2022 — WHILE ALL THIS IS HAPPENING, THE ROOM IS PROSECUTING HIM FOR AGE

The serious governance work runs parallel to one of UL's dumbest durable bits. In September 2020, Ansun calls him **`our beloved old man Snow`** and proposes a **`happy 50th birthday`**. Shiki answers **`I thought snow was 26`**. Gabu later calls him old man; Sye disputes the charge.

The prosecution kept changing its charge sheet. Rooks stranded Grandpa on **MySpace**, supposedly unable to operate modern apps; Snow answered **`shush`**. Cookie weighed in on whether he *sounded* old with **`he is old man`** and a snoring-grandpa GIF. Rich later accused Grandpa of **`misplacing things again`**, and by November 2022 even Snow's own in-game names were apparently escaping his memory. The joke had moved from birthday arithmetic to obsolete technology and the everyday indignities of forgetting things.

Snow was not merely enduring it. He supplied get-off-my-lawn material, **`Whipper snapper origin 1700`**, and Methuselah himself. By the 2026 reunion, the room had graduated from Grandpa to **ancient**, **dinosaur**, **Mesozoic buddy**, and **Babylonian tablets**. A fake fiftieth birthday had become an archaeological dig. The best part is that Snow could still answer a technical question in the middle of being treated like somebody who personally witnessed the invention of writing.

Snow is user-confirmed as older than much of the core group, Cantonese, formerly U.S. Air Force, and a cybersecurity professional working with hospital electronic medical record systems. Those real-world facts help explain why technical/privacy topics recur, but they do not get to swallow the biography.

## 2024–2026 — THE GAME FADES; THE SAME SNOW KEEPS ANSWERING THE ACTUAL QUESTION

By November 2024, the technical subject is no longer a Dragon Raja event bot. Snow answers a display-quality question by distinguishing a phone from a computer monitor: the difference is not meaningfully visible in the first context but can be noticeable in the second. In February 2025 he asks Mugen whether the old chats are archived or removed and listens while she explains the manual export process. The useful continuity is not that Snow always has the answer. It is that he keeps checking what the system actually does.

The 2026 Safe House reunion compresses the serious and unserious Snow into the same few weeks. He warns **`Bad practice. Don't click on links`**. He later says he was looking for a key to escape **`the demented`**. On March 10 he asks **`Do I need security clearance to enter this room? 😳`**, then traces the server's bot history from DiscordBot through Saber to the later bespoke system. Akariel answers **`Astonishing display of knowledge`**.

The security-clearance joke matters because it is Snow using his own vocabulary against himself. The same man who spent years talking about permissions and access can now walk into a Discord room and make the entire concept sound absurd with one wide-eyed emoji. The bot-history scene matters for the opposite reason: he does remember a technical lineage years later. Both can coexist with **`Forgot all the commands 😱`**. The archive does not need Snow to be infallible in order for him to be recognizably knowledgeable.

The longer UL progression is more specific than a role ladder: early fixer → room-boundary setter → conduct-framework drafter → collaborator willing to hand systems sideways → senior member whose own age becomes community property → later technical elder still asking questions, warning people about links, and turning security language into jokes. The Pit Boss / Judgemen titles name present roles. The timeline shows the person who made those roles feel plausible without falsely crowning him #2.

## 2022 — THE POKEOFFICER PROPOSAL

Snow's January 6 Snowfyre business card did not remain a business card for long. He proposed turning Officers into collectible `pokeofficer` cards that members could catch and redeem for prizes. It is a proposal, not a documented launch or prize payout. As an episode, though, it captures the collision between his formal Officer world and his willingness to make that same world into a ridiculous little game. The original `snowcard.jpg` still needs visual inspection.

## THE #FREESNOW CUSTODY HEARING — AUGUST 2026

Snow claimed Mugs was keeping him locked in the interrogation room. Mugen contributed a jail GIF instead of a defense. Oyasumi immediately filed **`#freesnow`**, and Moon took the prosecution's side with **`Get back in there you!`** and kidnapping GIFs. One man complained about captivity, one friend started a liberation campaign, and another apparently considered the prisoner a flight risk. This was QOTD clown court, not an actual detention, and the hashtag is documented here as a one-day joke rather than a standing movement. The scene adds a reunion-era echo of Snow's lifelong habit of treating authority and procedure as excellent raw material for nonsense.

## THE BEST MATERIAL WAS ALLEGEDLY IN VOICE CHAT

Snow had already tried a different loophole on October 30, 2020: **`you cant wall of shame me in the wall of shame room if I shamed myself first 😛`**. File the complaint against yourself and the prosecution has nothing left to do. He never explained what he was preemptively confessing to. Five days later, his next defense was to keep the premium nonsense in voice chat instead. Snow could help design a system and still spend his free time looking for ways to make it prosecute him less efficiently.

Shiya wanted Snow to type his nonsense where it could be screenshotted. Snow's answer was to claim his best material stayed in VC, safely out of reach of the Wall's usual paperwork. Anthos immediately reduced the supposedly premium content to one activity: **monch**. Zoshaa then arrived to prosecute the double standard, because Snow had apparently found time to criticize her for eating **ONE(1) FREEZIE**. It is a wonderfully Snow-shaped contradiction: he knows exactly how the evidence machine works, jokes about evading it, and gets caught in a completely different charge anyway. The room was teasing him about what happened in voice chat; nobody was legislating what people could eat.

## THE BUSHES CASE (OR: WHY DOES EVERYONE ALREADY KNOW?)

Gilli casually remembered the time Ana left Snow naked in bushes. Pride answered **`i have picture`** eight seconds later, and Zoshaa soon supplied the label **`raining nude bush hours`**. Nobody stopped to explain the scene. That is the joke: everybody in the room apparently knew which Snow disaster was being discussed, and the rest of us have been handed the police report without the crime scene.

The older nickname exchange identifies Ana as Anayss. The original incident and the alleged picture remain missing, so the November 2020 retelling stays a remembered episode rather than an invented origin story. MAIN tells this as part of Snow's larger habit of becoming Wall material; WIKI keeps the dated witness chain.

## OCTOBER 2020 — SNOW VOLUNTEERS AS WALL EVIDENCE

Snow told the Wall he had planned to give Sycee, Kuki, or Ana something worth posting. Anayss was so accustomed to his remarks that she had ignored this one; Sye complained she could not beat the others to the post. Snow then asked whether Ana's immunity or Ansun waiting in the wings was worse. Juicy Cookie settled the case with **both**. The actual remark that started the exchange is missing. Snow was both willing bait and an enthusiastic critic of his own press corps.

## PETTY CRIMES — FACEBOOK DAD MEMES

Before the room put Snow on trial for being approximately as old as civilization, Ansun had already reviewed his meme taste and found **`parent's facebook humour and stock images`**. Snow's entire defense was **`FINE`**. No elaborate rebuttal, no appeal to the Judgemen. Just a man accepting that his sense of humor had been assigned a parental generation against his will.

That August 2020 roast belongs beside the later Old Man Snow jokes, not as their invented birthday. The particular image Ansun was judging is still unidentified. The exchange itself is plenty: Snow could draft twenty-four charges and still lose a three-message case about Facebook memes.


## SABER'S LONGEST-RUNNING BAD IDEA

Snow gave Saber a recurring Saturday job in September 2020: **`Spank Mugs Booty`**. The bot dutifully assigned it **`m1k2w9`**. The funny part is how long the joke survived its author pressing Enter. Sye and Gabu later maintained that same scheduled object, and the bot was still announcing it in 2025. Snow's technical mischief became somebody else's maintenance chore, then somebody else's, until a throwaway gag had the longevity of municipal infrastructure. The actual Dragon Raja activity was **Defend Boss**. On October 24, Saber announced the joke-title event beginning, and less than a second later Sota yelled **`Go Defend Boss!`**, begging everybody to save Mugs while they were dying. The bot spoke fluent Snow; Sota translated it into something members could actually play. Nobody needs to pretend the title described literal behavior. The command itself is already a crime against sensible scheduling.
