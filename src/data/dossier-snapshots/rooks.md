# Rooks

Rooks is current **Staff**, but the role title is almost beside the point because the archive gives her a much more specific recurring job: somebody hands Rooks an allegation or an evidence bag, and Rooks somehow makes it harder to dismiss. The room can start by defending her. It can accuse her. It can expect her to catch the screenshot. By the end, Rooks is often doing part of the prosecution herself.

The first clean hearing lands on August 24, 2020. Ren opens with **`Rooks is INOCENT just like me`**. Seven seconds later Ansun objects: **`rooks is NOT`**. About a minute afterward, Rooks narrows her own defense to **`Well, my mind isnt innocent at least`**. Ansun answers **`your search history says otherwise`**. Eight minutes after Ren's opening defense, Ren himself has switched sides and says **`Rooks steal us all`**. Rooks answers: **`Yes`**.

That is why `Rooks is innocent` is not the character read. Innocence is house vocabulary — other people claim it too. The Rooks-specific movement is the litigation collapsing from inside the defense table. Somebody says she is innocent; peers object; Rooks starts adding caveats and eventually just confirms the charge.

September 30 runs the procedure again. Cookie announces **`NONE OF U INNOCENT`**. Rooks answers **`IM INNOCENT`**. A pooled Deleted User gives `BRUH`; Gilli pings Rooks with eyes; Gabu is separately claiming innocence too. Under pressure, Rooks revises the filing to **`My body is innocent not my mind`**. Again, the room supplies a binary and Rooks personally drafts the exception that ruins the clean acquittal.

By November, the system no longer needs a prosecutor. Rooks posts an attachment to the Wall captioned **`Shaming myself again`**. Ren answers **`LMAOO`** twelve seconds later. The image was not visually inspected, so the wiki does not pretend to know what is in Exhibit A. The caption already does enough: screenshot jail has become self-service.

March 2021 proves that this is no longer merely Rooks making jokes about herself. Mugen uses a true Discord Reply to a Rooks Wall attachment and says: **`I was gonna ss it lmao then I thought nah rooks wouldnt miss this for the world`**. Rooks answers **`uwu cant hide from me`** and posts another attachment. Baby Lyssa then says **`OH MY GOD`**. The crucial shift is expectation. Mugen has internalized Rooks as somebody who will catch Wall-worthy evidence without being formally assigned to do so.

That does not make Rooks an Archivist, Evidence Clerk, moderator, or screenshot officer. The joke works better without the fake title. She has acquired a social reputation: if there is evidence, Rooks probably does not need to be told twice.

There is a second, quieter contribution in the same early era. On August 31, Rooks writes **`Sooooo we’ll just say happy birthday each day till we get it right`**. The next morning Anthos directly thanks her for **`starting out the new channel with birthdays`**. This supports Rooks as an early carrier or kickoff contributor in the birthday-room genealogy. It does not prove she created the channel, had channel-management permissions, owned the birthday ritual, or that the unnamed room is technically the exact same Discord object later exported under another title. The archive gives her a content handoff, not the keys to Discord admin.

Then the 2026 reunion supplies a beautifully stupid sequel. Mugen recognizes Rooks immediately and remembers an engagement milestone. Rooks corrects the timeline forward by saying she is married and mentions that they got a dog. Those are direct volunteered life updates and stay proportionate. Then Rooks adds **`im also left handed`**. Moon's response is **`WITCHHHH`**. Rooks does not defend herself. She improves the charge: **`DOUBLE WITCH`**.

Six years after the first innocence hearing, that is the same social verb in a cleaner form. An allegation appears; Rooks makes it more prosecutable.

The archive contains plenty of ambient recognition around Rooks — good mornings, direct greetings, Jackbox return invitations, ordinary care language — but those remain support rather than padding. A person page does not get better simply because every time somebody said hello is pasted into it. The five strong jobs already form a progression: defended and self-incriminating; defended again and narrowing the plea; submitting her own Wall exhibit; becoming socially expected to catch evidence; returning years later and escalating a fresh charge against herself.

Rooks does not need a fake title. She has screenshot jail jurisprudence.
