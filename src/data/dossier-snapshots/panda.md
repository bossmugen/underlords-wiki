## YOU STOLE MY HEART. APPARENTLY THIS IS A DAYCARE RECEIPT.

Daycare catches the Panda–Eos situation doing something the older Wall snippets could only hint at: it keeps moving.

Before Daycare ever got the full declarations, Panda had already put Eos on the dance card. His July 13 WALTZ submission came captioned **`with my only @Eos`**. Eos had entered MAFIA independently that spring, so this was Panda choosing a counterpart in public, not somebody being assigned to stand beside him. The picture is still uninspected; the choice of words is plenty.

On October 31, 2021 Panda says the quiet part at full volume: **`like how much i love eos`**.

By January the affection has logistics. Panda wants to keep playing AI, but not without Eos. Two days later he is taking Eos to Paris, announcing **`we movin there`**, and talking about dolls he bought for the two of them. Declaration becomes itinerary becomes merchandise. Very efficient relationship administration.

February gives the useful little correction. Panda says he has known Eos for almost three years. Eos answers **`3? or u mean 2`**. They are both counting; nobody gave the archive permission to pick the prettier number.

March is less subtle. Panda tells Eos **`because you're married to me`**. A week later Eos answers Panda's single-status argument with **`i mean... you stole my heart a long time ago`**. Panda posts a song, says it makes him think of Eos every time, and says he wants to sing it for Eos one day. Eos's response is basically a blush happening in public.

That is enough. UL marriage language stays UL marriage language; nobody is issuing a real-world marriage certificate or extracting sensitive biography from it. The useful part is already sitting in the verbs: Panda declares, plans, buys, dedicates. Eos corrects, answers, blushes back.

## BODY-SWAPPING DENIED ON ADMINISTRATIVE GROUNDS

Some Biodata arrives because a channel asks for it. Some arrives because somebody gives Panda a hypothetical and he refuses to cooperate with the premise.

The question is basically: **if you could be somebody else for a day, would you?**

Panda's answer starts surprisingly grounded: he says he has a very easy time being himself and does not want to be somebody else. Then the real character beat lands. Why would he put in the effort to fill somebody else's life when the arrangement only lasts a day?

**`far too much trouble`**.

The tiny shrug inside that sentence is better than an adjective list. He is not performing a tortured identity crisis. He is rejecting one on workload grounds.

The screenshot catches him under the display name **`ALHAITHAM (PANDA)`**, which adds exactly the right amount of costume to a statement whose underlying thesis is essentially: *I already have a perfectly serviceable self; please do not assign me extra admin.*

That is the kind of person-page receipt UL needs more of. Not `Panda is confident`. Not `Panda is lazy`. The archive gives us the gesture itself: somebody offers him a whole alternate existence and he starts calculating the inconvenience.

## WELCOME HOME, NOW WASH THE DISHES

Ghoulie greeted Panda's return with **“Welcome home, panda”** on June 4, 2021. Panda answered with a crying-panda emote and a complaint about being away so long. By July 7 the welcome had deteriorated into a fake kitchen dispute: Ghoulie accused Panda of not cleaning; Panda said the dishes were hers; Ghoulie pointed out his food was on them; Panda countered that she cooked it. Nobody volunteered to do the dishes. The whole argument happened on the Wall, where both already knew how to make themselves everybody else's problem.

The language is household comedy, not a shared-address record. It does show the ease with which these two could pick up a familiar argument and keep it going without explaining the bit to the room.
