## BODY-SWAPPING DENIED ON ADMINISTRATIVE GROUNDS

Some Biodata arrives because a channel asks for it. Some arrives because somebody gives Panda a hypothetical and he refuses to cooperate with the premise.

The question is basically: **if you could be somebody else for a day, would you?**

Panda's answer starts surprisingly grounded: he says he has a very easy time being himself and does not want to be somebody else. Then the real character beat lands. Why would he put in the effort to fill somebody else's life when the arrangement only lasts a day?

**`far too much trouble`**.

The tiny shrug inside that sentence is better than an adjective list. He is not performing a tortured identity crisis. He is rejecting one on workload grounds.

The screenshot catches him under the display name **`ALHAITHAM (PANDA)`**, which adds exactly the right amount of costume to a statement whose underlying thesis is essentially: *I already have a perfectly serviceable self; please do not assign me extra admin.*

That is the kind of person-page receipt UL needs more of. Not `Panda is confident`. Not `Panda is lazy`. The archive gives us the gesture itself: somebody offers him a whole alternate existence and he starts calculating the inconvenience.
