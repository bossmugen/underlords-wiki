# Korea

Korea is reaction-native without being all reaction. She notices the little details, the room's routines, and the people who make a familiar space feel occupied.

## The name has to look right

She asked for the brackets around 『KOREA』 because plain KOREA looked wrong. The request was exact, the delivery was gentle, and she made it easy to say no. That combination is more revealing than a generic claim that she likes aesthetics.

## The room has a rhythm

She noticed when Ghoulie's daily Wall posts stopped appearing. She also thanked the room when she had to leave for work, and Eos noticed her exit. Korea's attention moves both ways: she notices the room, and the room notices her.
