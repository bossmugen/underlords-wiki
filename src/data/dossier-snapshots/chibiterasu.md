# Chibiterasu

**Chibiterasu** is the in-game name attached to the stable account the room also knows as **Chubi**, while current canon keeps **Chuutan / Chuu~tan / Chuubi / Chibi** in the same alias family. Chibiterasu is now a Game Officer and Parterre Gardener, but the surviving direct material is still much better at showing identity continuity than appointment history or a giant personality profile. The biography should therefore stay smaller than the loudest cast files without feeling like an empty role card.

In March 2022, the Lobby's Bouncer asks the usual intake questions. The stable `chubita._` account, displayed as Chubi, answers with **`IGN: chibiterasu`** and **`Club: Underlords`**. The historical age field is not needed publicly. Sye later true-replies that the role has been assigned and points Chubi toward the Discord Tour. That one scene gives the archive a clean bridge between the casual Discord name and the name that now heads the character file.

A year later, Chubi appears somewhere more social than paperwork. Mugen's March 31, 2023 anniversary wrap-up congratulates the event's **`Nitro Snipers`** and names `@Chubi` in the list. The announcement thanks the broader group for anniversary greetings, messages, presents, art, posters and banners, but it does not identify which exact contribution qualified Chubi. The safe claim is participation and public recognition inside the anniversary ritual. `Nitro Sniper` is the announcement's joke/event label; it does not move Chibi onto the formal Sniper shelf or authorize the wiki to invent an unseen artifact.

Two years after the first stable-account check-in, the same account is carded again. This time the answer begins **`Chuu~tan, Underlords`** and ends with a Cat_Drool emote. Sye follows with **`chu chu`**. The exchange is tiny, but it does exactly enough: Chuu~tan is not an unrelated new person created by punctuation, and the human response carries a little familiarity that the generic Bouncer card cannot. The form is asking for identity; the room is already playing with the name.

That is currently the honest progression. Chubi self-identifies as Chibiterasu in 2022; the same social name is publicly recognized in a 2023 anniversary ritual; the same account later presents Chuu~tan in 2024; the room answers in familiar shorthand; current roster canon places all of those names under one Game Officer / Parterre Gardener file. None of that tells us when either appointment began, and the gardening title is not permission to invent a flower obsession, a real-world hobby, or a personality made out of horticultural metaphors.

So Chibiterasu remains one of the intentionally restrained biographies. The quirks that survive are the ones actually present in the language — the alias drift, the Cat_Drool punctuation, Sye's little **`chu chu`**, and the fact that UL can temporarily call her a Nitro Sniper without the archive losing track of her real shelf — rather than traits reverse-engineered from a badge. More ordinary-life scenes can deepen the person later. Until then, keeping one person intact across several names and several systems is already worthwhile archive work.
