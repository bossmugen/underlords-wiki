# Noether

Noether is a Game Officer, but the title explains very little about the woman who could turn a dessert complaint into an entire judicial system and then show up with actual cake. Give her one ridiculous premise and she'll extend it until somebody else starts using her vocabulary. The funniest part is how seriously she can sound while doing it.

## The Sugar Court Has No Appeals

With HamitteY, Noether developed an impressive commitment to prosecuting food crimes that nobody had actually committed. Anayss started an `Unsugar hami` bit; Noether contributed enormous keyboard-spilling laughter, `50 gold`, and `Unsugar hamihami`. Soon the room had `Unsugared snow`, `Salty Ham`, and a sugar economy with absolutely no responsible adult in charge.

By February 2022, HamitteY was on trial for allegedly withholding dessert. Noether's response to a defense was simply `Lies`. She could declare that she didn't judge dessert preferences and then immediately announce that Hami was not a good option. It is a spectacularly short journey from impartial judge to personal vendetta. Later, when the joke swung around and accused *her*, she pleaded `Im innocent , hamcito dont believe in me`. Eos picked up the hamcito verdict too. Noether was perfectly happy to be a defendant if it meant the bit could keep going.

And she wasn't all imaginary pastry law. She announced strawberry ice cream after complaining about unshared desserts. Anayss kept asking what she would cook for everybody; Noether answered with bread, pizza, chocolate cake, focaccia, and cupcakes in chocolate, lemon, and apple-walnut. She also posted a *completo italiano* recipe in QOTD and followed it with `Completo ❤️`. The woman had a menu ready whenever somebody gave her an excuse.

## Anayss Made Room

Noether remembered Anayss welcoming her when she felt alone and was losing interest in playing. That kindness stayed with her. Their exchanges became affectionate, full of sunlight-colored compliments and requests to cook for the whole room. Anayss wasn't just another person laughing at the sugar court; she was someone Noether could thank for making the place feel worth returning to.

In July 2022, Anayss announced that she'd finally met Noether in person, and that Noether had baked her a cake. Noether called it `A date with ana`. The playful wording does not need a romantic plot attached. After months of joking about feeding everyone, she had brought something made with her own hands to someone who had made her feel welcome. That is a much better payoff than another fictional dessert sentence.

## Familiar Faces, Terrible Paperwork

The automated Lobby greetings were less emotionally intelligent than the humans. Noether's account was greeted more than once, while Anayss responded with `wb ❤️` and Mugen with `Welcome back noeee`. The bot treated the moment as another introduction; the people recognized the person who had come back.

Noether's warmth could be tiny as well as theatrical. In a later Wall exchange she posted a Cheer sticker and wrote `He said plz so cute` with a heart. Who the `he` was isn't clear, but the reaction is unmistakably hers: the same woman who could condemn a friend with one word could be delighted by somebody saying please.

## Petty Crimes

- Turns other people's food jokes into a full vocabulary, then volunteers as the defendant.
- Has used `Lies` as a complete dessert-law argument.
- Responds to imaginary catering requests with suspiciously specific cupcake flavors.
- Posted a *completo italiano* recipe; posting it is not the same as claiming she invented or photographed it.
- Actually brought cake to Anayss after months of talking about what she might bake.

Noether's recurring trick is to take the unserious seriously enough that other people can join in. She makes room for another joke, another flavor, another return. Sometimes the punchline is a cake that really exists.
