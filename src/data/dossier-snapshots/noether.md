# Noether

Noether is a current **Game Officer**, not a current Sniper. The role is firm; the appointment date is not. More importantly, the archive does not need a promotion receipt to make Noether feel like a person. The strongest surviving run is almost aggressively non-institutional: long messy laughter, fake verdicts, sugar taxonomy, dessert accusations, being prosecuted right back, and then the absurdly satisfying moment where mock scarcity turns into actual food.

The stable `noether88` account checks into Lobby on October 28, 2021 with Noether and Underlords. The historical age field is unnecessary to the public biography. Anayss assigns the role and points Noether toward the usual rooms. Two months later MEE6 cards the same account again, but Anayss answers **`@Noether wb ❤️`**. On January 13 the machinery fires yet again and Mugen says **`Welcome back noeee`**. The bot keeps trying to introduce the account; the humans keep supplying continuity. Those return beats keep the October card from being promoted into a magical first-membership date.

Between those door scenes, Noether has already started becoming recognizable inside the Wall's own grammar. On November 2, Anayss throws out **`Unsugar hami`** and Noether's authored run includes **`Ahhaahahahahahahahaah`**, **`50 gold`**, more laughter later, and **`Unsugar hamihami`**. The exact spelling of a laugh is not a diagnosis, but the repetition is useful voice evidence: Noether often lets amusement arrive as a long phonetic keyboard spill, then snaps back into compact fake accounting.

By December, the vocabulary has expanded into **`Unsugared snow, history of a sugar daddy and unsugar boiii`**. None of that is financial or romantic evidence; it is the room turning nonsense into a fake economy and Noether proving very willing to keep adding categories once the premise exists. The fun is not only that the terms are stupid. Noether treats the category system as if it deserves another subclause.

By February 2022 the joke has migrated from sugar to dessert. Noether accuses HamitteY of not sharing desserts, answers one objection with the magnificently efficient **`Lies`**, and later writes **`Yes, I do not judge preference in dessert, but hami is not a good option`**. That sentence performs neutrality for half a second and then immediately resumes the prosecution. It is Noether's mock-serious register in miniature: take an unserious dispute, briefly give it a principle, then use the principle to make the dispute worse.

Later the same day comes **`I made strawberry ice cream, since you didn't share desserts`** with an image attachment. The attachment remains visually undescribed here, and the 23-minute-52-second gap between Discord messages is not a cooking timer. The useful change is social rather than culinary: the person prosecuting dessert scarcity is now the person introducing dessert into the conversation.

The pattern becomes recognizable to other people too. On July 7, Anayss tags Noether and HamitteY together and writes **`ty for so much material`**. Noether is not content to remain the prosecutor; the same Wall run eventually gets **`Im innocent , hamcito dont believe in me`** with crying emotes. The defense is still participation. Noether does not step outside the joke to protest being included; the innocence plea simply becomes another exhibit.

Three weeks later the dessert thread leaves Discord entirely. Anayss posts in Slice: **`Today I met @Noether! She baked a cake for me ❤️`**. Noether replies **`A date with ana`**. The wording is playful, not a romance receipt; the location and wider circumstances stay private. What the archive can safely say is already better: one of the people previously encountered through Lobby cards and Wall nonsense became somebody Anayss met offline, and Noether brought cake.

The late archive is thinner, but December 2023 keeps the voice from being trapped inside Dessert Character forever. Anayss again tags Noether and HamitteY into a Wall exchange; later Noether posts a Cheer sticker and writes **`He said plz so cute`** with a heart emote. The source excerpt does not safely resolve who `he` is, so the subject stays unresolved. The reaction itself is enough: after all the fake law and `Lies`, Noether can also be caught noticing one tiny polite word and finding it adorable.

That is the progression worth keeping. Noether moves from identifiable member → repeatedly recognized returner → active participant who can take somebody else's joke premise and keep building on it → mock-serious dessert prosecutor → defendant inside the same Wall machinery → person whose recurring dessert language starts producing actual strawberry ice cream in the record → offline UL relationship made ordinary enough to involve cake → later warm reaction that has nothing to do with dessert at all. The current Game Officer role sits beside that biography rather than swallowing it. The missing background stays missing, and the quirks stay proportionate: enough recurrence to make Noether distinctive, nowhere near enough to sentence the person to a permanent career as UL's pastry department.
