# Rose

## THE ACCOUNT SAYS DARKASRAI. THE INTAKE SAYS ROSE.

The stable Discord account gives us the bridge cleanly: `darkasrai` / `DarkAsrai` self-files as **R O S E** in 2021. Mugs confirms Rose is a woman and Staff in the archive-era roster.

That does not give us appointment chronology. It gives us the person.

## RETURN RECOGNIZED BEFORE THE PAPERWORK

November 7, 2022:

> `Hi gilli`

Gilli answers one second later:

> `Welcome back rose!`

Then asks her to repeat the intake fields so she can assign the role.

The useful order is recognition first, paperwork second. The scene gives Rose a return without requiring a made-up departure date to explain it.

## WALL BEHAVIOR — THE RECEIPTS ARE ALSO A SPORT

**Defendant awareness.** Rose can already predict herself becoming Wall material: **`I knew id get in here`**.

**Filing competition.** Two days later she says **`Beat me to it`**, then posts her own two-image packet roughly two minutes afterward. Being scooped does not end her participation; she still wants her exhibits in the file.

**Playful challenge.** On New Year's Day she turns old game context into **`Bet wanna fight me XD`**, then immediately frames it as a rust check: **`Lets see how much i remember of the combat`**. Rummy true-replies **`Yesh- gonna be very swuiShy`**. The useful relationship detail is the fast shared game register, not a formal rival label.

**Premise escalation.** Xuseio later tags Rose and Cookie while confessing that he posted a screenshot because he thought it said **`children`**. Rose and Cookie wheeze; Rose follows with **`Noo they taste like chicken XD`**; Xuseio and Moon wheeze back. Rose's role in the bit is clear: given an absurd misunderstanding, she makes the misunderstanding worse instead of restoring order.

**Running contradiction.** Rose is organized enough to keep and submit receipts, but the organization is not in service of solemn accuracy inside the joke. She uses competent evidence-handling for competitive filing and deliberate nonsense.

## THE KITCHEN ANSWER HAS TOO MUCH EQUIPMENT TO BE CASUAL VIBES

QOTD later asks for a dream kitchen and Rose starts specifying capacity: industrial cold storage, blast chiller, dry-aging cabinet, double convection oven, large work surfaces, baking storage. Months later, when Gabu asks what recipe people would teach her, Rose chooses profiteroles / cream puffs / choux à la crème.

That is enough to call serious cooking/baking interest. It is not enough to call Rose a chef.
