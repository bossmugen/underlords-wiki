# Pride

## Cast

Pride was perfectly capable of warning newcomers that the Wall caught everybody, then making themself a repeat defendant. The house's preferred response was a screenshot; Pride's preferred response was a sip and an appeal against the charge.

## Relationships

**Gilli** kept the Wall's filing machinery close enough to Pride that the threat of exposure became a running conversation. Pride answered the warning with a dare rather than a retreat.

**Ren** supplied the most extravagant retrospective: after an earlier Club Chat disaster repeatedly lost Pride's body, Ren claimed she had made an Amber Alert for the missing corpse. **Illien** remembered the repeated lost-body announcements, and Pride was still laughing about that day in November.

## Running Gags

**The corpse requires municipal services.** Illien remembered seeing `we've lost Pride's body` repeatedly in Club Chat. Ren's answer was apparently missing-person infrastructure for a corpse. Pride's `I still laugh about that day` is what turns the retrospective from somebody else's accusation into a joke the subject still owns. The original incident and Amber Alert image remain unrecovered.

**Sip, then appeal.** Pride could warn people that everybody was being watched and then answer their own Wall charges with `i regret nothing` or insist that a shame moment was a proud moment. The defendant was apparently also the appeals court.
