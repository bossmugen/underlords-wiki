# Suzimasu

Founder. Historical Consigliere. Senior Advisor in civilian translation.

## OFFICIAL FILE

Suzimasu belongs to the definitive founding five: Mugen, Ritha, WolfPhenix, Suzimasu, HyaLuna.

His later institutional history includes service as a full Officer in the **Consigliere / Senior Advisor** lane. That is retired Officer history, not a reason to backdate the title onto every early message.

## IDENTITY

The archive cycles through **Mob**, **BAPE**, **Mob | BAPE**, and `mob_bape`. An early lobby bot interaction is especially useful provenance: Gilli targets Mob | BAPE and the bot renders the recipient as **Suzimasu**.

One person. Several names. No duplicate founder required.

## FOUR TABS AND DINNER

April 15, 2020. The founder file briefly stops looking like an org chart because `mob_bape` explains the problem:

> **Yeah my bad currently trying to play yakuza 0 and dragon and text you guys lol**

Nineteen seconds later:

> **And get my dinner on**

That is not Officer work and the page will not dress it up as Officer work. It is more useful than that. UL is barely out of its opening month and one of the five people in the origin story is already visible as an ordinary person with too many things open at once. **Yakuza 0. Dragon Raja. The group chat. Dinner.** Founder status apparently did not come with additional hands.

The next nearby Training Arena announcement belongs to the pooled Deleted User account, not Suzimasu. The chronology stays where Discord put it; authorship does not migrate because two messages happened in the same room.

## THE BOUNCER THINKS THIS IS AN ARRIVAL. REN DOES NOT.

May 19, 2021. MEE6 / **Underlords Stripclub Bouncer** fires the ordinary intake script at the same stable `mob_bape` account:

> **Hey @Mob | BAPE, welcome to Underlords! Pls state your ign / club and age for role assignment. Thanks**

Thirty-eight seconds later Ren does not ask who this is.

> **UwU Sushiii**

Mob answers with a bee-daikon emote. Ren:

> **Ehehehe**

The bot is acting like the front door has a new customer. The room is not. That asymmetry is the useful part: software can restart procedure; social recognition does not necessarily restart with it.

It does **not** tell us why the Bouncer fired. No departure/rejoin chronology, no role-loss story, no appointment reset. And `Sushiii` stays what the receipt actually proves: Ren's address for Suzimasu in this scene, not a new legal identity because one extra vowel had a good day.

## FILE STATUS

Founder and retired Officer status: locked. Alias chain: archive-supported and user-confirmed. Full dated Officer appointment/departure sequence: not reconstructed.

The newer receipts add something the old file lacked: the founding-era title and the person wearing it are allowed to exist at the same time. One is Consigliere. The other is trying to get dinner without closing Yakuza 0.
