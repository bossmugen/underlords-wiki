# Suzimasu

Suzimasu belongs to UL's origin story as part of the five-person founding circle with Mugen, Ritha, WolfPhenix, and HyaLuna. Later he served as a full Officer in the Consigliere / Senior Advisor lane and is now retired from that role. The appointment chronology is incomplete, so the biography keeps the founding layer, the later Officer layer, and the person between them distinct.

## APRIL 2020 — THE ARCHIVE RESOLVES THE NAMES, THEN IMMEDIATELY SHOWS THE HUMAN PROBLEM

Early Lobby evidence directly bridges **Mob | BAPE** to Suzimasu, while later records also use Mob, BAPE, and `mob_bape`. The identity chain matters because otherwise the opening-era person would fragment into several fake founders.

On April 15, the stable `mob_bape` account explains what he is actually doing with his evening: trying to play **Yakuza 0**, play **Dragon Raja**, text the UL room, and get dinner at the same time.

That scene does not need to be dressed up as leadership. Its value is precisely the opposite. UL is barely out of its first month and one of the people in the founding story is visible with too many tabs open and not enough hands. Founder status did not protect anyone from needing dinner.

## MAY 2021 — THE BOT FORGETS THE SOCIAL HISTORY; REN DOES NOT

A year later, the Stripclub Bouncer fires the ordinary intake welcome at the same stable Mob | BAPE account as though a new arrival has appeared. Thirty-eight seconds later Ren simply greets **`UwU Sushiii`**. Mob answers with an emote; Ren answers **`Ehehehe`**.

The exchange does not prove a leave/rejoin cycle, role loss, or reappointment. It proves something narrower and more useful: account-state machinery can restart procedure while human recognition continues uninterrupted.

That is the small progression the current archive supports. Suzimasu enters as one of the founding friends, appears in ordinary 2020 house life under the Mob/BAPE identity, remains socially recognizable across a later bot re-check, and eventually has a separate history as a full Officer / Senior Advisor.

There is not enough direct later material to invent a grander personality arc. The bio does not need one. Founding origin → opening-era person → social continuity → later Officer service is already a real timeline.
