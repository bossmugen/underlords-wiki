# Suzimasu

Suzimasu belongs to UL's origin story as part of the five-person founding circle with Mugen, Ritha, WolfPhenix, and HyaLuna. Later he served as a full Officer in the Consigliere / Senior Advisor lane and is now retired from that role. The appointment chronology is incomplete, so the biography keeps the founding layer, the later Officer layer, and the person between them distinct.

The cleanest identity chain is **Suzimasu = Mob = BAPE = Mob | BAPE = `mob_bape`**. That matters because the early archive uses the account-name language more often than the later canonical name. Without the bridge, one person would fracture into several fake characters and his earliest ordinary scenes would stop attaching to the founder they actually belong to.

## APRIL 2020 — THE ARCHIVE RESOLVES THE NAMES, THEN IMMEDIATELY SHOWS THE HUMAN PROBLEM

Early Lobby evidence directly bridges **Mob | BAPE** to Suzimasu. On April 15, the stable `mob_bape` account explains what he is actually doing with his evening: trying to play **Yakuza 0**, play **Dragon Raja**, text the UL room, and get dinner at the same time.

That scene does not need to be dressed up as leadership. Its value is precisely the opposite. UL is barely out of its first month and one of the people in the founding story is visible with too many tabs open and not enough hands. Founder status did not protect anyone from needing dinner. The scene gives the early institutional chart a human scale: whatever responsibilities Suzimasu will later hold, the archive first catches him as somebody attempting several forms of entertainment, conversation, and basic feeding at once.

## APRIL 2021 — WHO ALL ARE GEMINI. APPARENTLY THIS REQUIRES EXORCISM.

On April 14, Milk announces that the fellow Geminis are here to `FUCK SHIET UP~`. Suzimasu's first response is not an astrology essay. It is **`Wait who all are Gemini`**. Then **`>.>`**. Roughly ninety seconds later, he posts a playful begone-demon / exorcism / garlic GIF.

The sequence is useful because the joke lives in pacing. First: obtain the list. Second: stare at the list. Third: apparently deploy spiritual pest control. The archive does not support treating this as a literal belief about astrology or religion. It supports a much smaller and funnier social behavior: Suzimasu hears a ridiculous premise, asks one practical question, and then chooses an equally ridiculous reaction once the premise has had time to land.

## MAY 2021 — THE BOT FORGETS THE SOCIAL HISTORY; REN DOES NOT

A month later, the Stripclub Bouncer fires the ordinary intake welcome at the same stable Mob | BAPE account as though a new arrival has appeared. Thirty-eight seconds later Ren simply greets **`UwU Sushiii`**. Mob answers with a bee-daikon emote; Ren answers **`Ehehehe`**.

The exchange does not prove a leave/rejoin cycle, role loss, or reappointment. It proves something narrower and more useful: account-state machinery can restart procedure while human recognition continues uninterrupted. The bot needs a form. Ren needs approximately one second of social memory. Suzimasu does not answer with a résumé or explanation; an emote is enough to keep the exchange moving.

## JULY 2021 — APOLOGY FIRST. OFFENSE SECOND. CONTEXT LOST TO HISTORY.

On July 2, Anayss posts `:OkButWhy:`. Suzimasu then writes **`I’m sorry @New Account Gilli#3226`**, posts the `Missyshy_donotsteal` emote, and follows with **`I HAD TO TRY IT LMAOOOO`**.

The missing parent context matters. The wiki cannot safely say what the emote did, what exactly Suzimasu was trying, or why Gilli deserved warning. But the surviving order is almost enough on its own: apology, action, delighted confession. He knows before he does the thing that Gilli may reasonably require an apology; this knowledge does not prevent the thing; afterward he is laughing too hard to pretend it was accidental.

That is character evidence without requiring a made-up psychological profile. In this scene, Suzimasu is playful enough to commit to a bit, socially aware enough to warn the target, and delighted enough by the result to immediately admit intent. It also gives the 2021 file a warmer relational texture than the role chart ever could. Gilli and Anayss are not being used to manufacture a closeness ranking. They are simply the people present when Suzimasu decides an advance apology counts as adequate risk management.

## LATER ROLE HISTORY — IMPORTANT, BUT NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR THE PERSON

Locked roster canon later records Suzimasu as a full Officer in the historical Consigliere / Senior Advisor lane. That belongs on the page because it is real UL history. It does not give us permission to reverse-engineer an appointment date, a retirement date, or a personality from the title. `Consigliere` does not automatically mean quiet strategist, diplomat, disciplinarian, therapist, or shadow deputy. Those would each require their own evidence.

The surviving person-first material is more modest and therefore more useful. The founding friend who is trying to play two games while getting dinner is recognizably the same sort of social participant who, a year later, asks who all the Geminis are before escalating the joke, gets recognized by Ren without needing to re-introduce himself, and apologizes to Gilli before doing the unexplained thing anyway. The archive gives us ordinary presence, comic timing, and continuity before it gives us a grand theory of Suzimasu.

There is still not enough direct later material to invent a complete personality arc. The current source set does not safely establish his profession, hometown, broader offline biography, exact Officer appointment, exact retirement, or what his day-to-day Senior Advisor work looked like. Those gaps stay open until better evidence appears. Founding origin → opening-era ordinary life → socially recognizable 2021 presence → later Officer service is already a real timeline. It does not need fanfic poured into the spaces between.
