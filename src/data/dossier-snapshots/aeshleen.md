## SHE QUIT THE GAME, NOT THE GROUP CHAT

Aeshleen gives Slice one of its cleanest portability receipts. She tells Dragon Raja **`it's over`**; UL responds like the divorce decree somehow forgot to include custody of the people. Then the archive catches her months later casually filing goats, art exhibits, picnics, river swims, and a village soap-box race back into Slice.

The goodbye was to the game. The group chat apparently did not accept service.

## THE BROKEN-HEART EMOJI WAS NOT A RESIGNATION LETTER

In April 2021 Aeshleen hands the archive the operating note herself: **`making jokes is my way to cope.`** A year later, Wall gives us a scene where that sentence stops being abstract and starts moving around in public.

On May 10, 2022, the surviving sequence catches Aeshleen going **`WHAT`**, then **`lemme alone 💔`**, then **`stop bringing up my past 💔`**. Rich answers the last one by trying to settle damages in **Luffy's hat**, because One Piece merchandise had already become recognized legal tender in Aeshleen court.

Then the useful part: roughly half an hour later Aeshleen comes back with **`bAHAHHA is oki ILY yall`**. Ren replies directly: **`WE LOVE U MOREEEE`**.

That turn is the character evidence. The broken-heart language performs injury loudly; the return message dissolves the scene into laughter and collective affection; Ren immediately meets her in the same oversized register.

## THE FANDOM CONTEXT WILL BE ENTERED INTO EVIDENCE

The Wall repeatedly catches Aeshleen trying to repair the room's interpretation of whatever it has just preserved. In 2023: **`for the record. we were talking about law, zoro, robin, boa and luffy.`** In 2024: **`FOR THE CONTEXT`** after naming the current fandom discussion.

Mugs's background lock makes the recurring One Piece material less accidental: Aeshleen is a French woman, a historical Staff member, a One Piece fan, and somebody who loves Mugen. The archive does not need to invent more intimacy than that. It already has enough to explain why a Luffy hat can appear in the middle of a Wall repair scene and still make immediate social sense.
