# Scar

## Cast

Scar is a permanent Platelet. The Lobby introduction is short enough to fit on a label: \`Scar ; Council ; guest\`. On the Wall, Scar quickly gets comfortable with a different kind of label-making: put a theory into circulation, let everybody else argue with it, then return later as if the room has just confirmed the obvious.

## Relationships

### Gilli

Scar's August 2021 theory begins with \`Also Gilli must have some harem here\`. Gilli answers in full caps that she does not. Scar's reply is not a retreat or a long defense: \`Why not you?\` The room joins in, and Scar comes back hours later with \`I knew it all time\`. Gilli gets to deny the charge; Scar gets to keep the joke alive. Nobody is being assigned a real romantic history.

### Ansun

Ansun gives Scar a quick \`youre right\` during the same pile-on. It is a tiny vote of confidence in the bit, not a formal alliance or a literal endorsement of the accusation.

### Cookie and Tofu

In October, Cookie's \`LIES\` gets Scar's direct \`want me to exposed?\` reply. Tofu is part of the preceding \`IT’S TRUE\` exchange, although the original proposition is not clear enough to reconstruct. The useful connection is the shared Wall vocabulary, not an invented case file.

## Episode — Gilli's Alleged Harem

**August 19, 2021 · Wall**

Scar throws the theory into the room, Gilli rejects it, and Scar immediately turns the denial into another opening: \`Why not you?\` Ansun backs the joke; the room supplies the rest of the prosecution. Scar returns later with \`I knew it all time\`. The scene works because nobody needs to take the premise seriously for everyone to understand the game.

## Running Gag — The Wall as a Verb

By October, \`exposed\` can do the work of a whole sentence. Scar's \`want me to exposed?\` is short, misspelled, and perfectly legible to the people in the room. It shows familiarity with the Wall's receipt culture without proving that Scar actually had an image ready to post.
