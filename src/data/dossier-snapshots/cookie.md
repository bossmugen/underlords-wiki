# Cookie

Cookie. Kuki. Cookies and Juicebox. One person, several snacks, and eventually a recurring legal strategy: **deny everything and become a running panda**.

The earliest clean defense lands on September 30, 2020. A pooled Deleted User says **`kuki saw too much today`**. Cookie says **`NO`**. Gilli contributes **`F for kuki`**. Later that day Cookie files a narrower brief: **`im innocent, not horny`**.

`Innocent` is not Cookie property. UL had several defendants making the same claim in the same era. The Cookie-specific texture is his location inside the argument: he is witness, participant, immediate denier, and still there long enough to keep answering.

May 2021 gives the file a completely different kind of complaint. Cookie asks **`yall playing VR`**, adds a question mark, then says **`i wasnt invited`** and follows with a crying emoji. No scandal. No Wall prosecution. Just a small claim on the group's shared play: *you started the thing and I expected to be part of the thing*. It is belonging expressed as grievance, which is approximately how a Discord server becomes family without anybody filing paperwork.

By October the Wall has learned how to prosecute Cookie and Cookie has learned how to leave the jurisdiction without actually leaving the room. On October 5 Tofu posts: **`I'm not saying @Cookies and Juicebox is a cochino but, he's a cochino.`** There is a screenshot attachment; the binary was not safely inspected, so the wiki does not invent its contents. Cookie's answer requires no image description: **`:PandaRun:`**.

Three days later the defense has become a procedure. Cookie appears with **`:PandaRun:`**, then **`its not how it looks like`**, then **`i swear`**. Tofu posts another screenshot attachment. Cookie answers with another **`:PandaRun:`** and **`so done`**. The next day: **`LIES`**, immediately followed by **`:PandaRun:`**.

That repetition across separate dates is why the panda matters. It is not one emote pasted after one awkward joke. The Wall presents an exhibit; Cookie disputes the exhibit; the panda leaves the scene. Then Cookie comes back later and the court reconvenes because apparently fleeing in place is legally valid in UL.

The important contradiction is that **escape never becomes departure**. Cookie is still there the next day to yell `LIES`, still there in later Wall scenes, and still visible in 2022. The emote performs retreat while the person remains socially reachable for the next allegation. That is a better character read than calling him scandalous or chaotic.

April 2022 supplies one perfect exception clause. Cookie says **`Man leave the goats alone xD`**. Eleven seconds later he adds **`unless we talking about Ganyu here`**. No thesis required. Cookie personally invalidated his own universal rule almost before the ink dried.

Then December 2022 returns to the participation/access thread. Cookie has forgotten Slice exists. Ren explains why with the brutally administrative **`Vip don’t got access here`**. A year earlier Cookie was protesting because VR started without him; now there is an entire room sitting behind a permission wall and he has apparently been outside long enough for the room itself to fall out of RAM.

The scene does **not** mean Cookie was excluded from UL socially, and it does not provide his complete VIP history. Current canon places him on Staff, while old export-time arrays cannot date promotions or explain every access change. What the exchange does show is funny enough: Cookie keeps discovering UL architecture by finding a door closed, then asking why he is not currently standing on the other side.

Those two threads fit without needing a generic personality label. In one, Cookie protests the evidence and performs escape without going anywhere. In the other, Cookie protests missing the shared activity or room because being part of it matters enough to complain. The defendant remains at large because the defendant keeps returning to the courthouse.
