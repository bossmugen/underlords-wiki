Crystalia arrived at the February welcome desk to a Mugen greeting that sounded like she had been expecting him. Nine days later he had already put a picture into the Lonely Photoshoot. By the following weekend, she was calling him into Wall jokes as UL's mermaid and giving him a fake Pool Boy television franchise.
