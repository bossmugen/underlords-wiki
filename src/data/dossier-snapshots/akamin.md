# Akamin

Akamin had the sort of entrance that made other people start performing hospitality. She showed up after skating in April 2020, just as Gilli had been asking after her. Snow immediately declared **`the bar is open for Akamin ^_^`**. Akamin's **`Snow baby heyyyy`** gave him exactly the audience he wanted; the imaginary establishment promptly produced a Mai Tai. Apparently she didn't need to request special treatment. She just had to arrive.

By December, Mugen was announcing that Akamin was scheduled to stream Dragon Raja throwbacks on Twitch. It puts her somewhere beyond the old roster names: somebody with a life outside the chat, familiar enough for Gilli to notice her absence, and whose game nostalgia Mugs thought worth gathering people around. Whether that particular broadcast went ahead is still unknown.
