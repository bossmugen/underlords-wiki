# Ame

Ame's messages are useful before they're elaborate. The warm greeting and the practical next step tend to arrive together.

## ROLE — GAME OFFICER

Ame is a current Game Officer. Keep that as a present roster fact; the public scenes below show how he makes the role feel, not when the appointment began.

## NOTICES THAT LEAVE ROOM FOR SOMEBODY ELSE

Ame's public messages have a useful little rhythm: the greeting and the practical thing arrive together. **"buena semana a tod@s ❤️"**, then an offer of two partner-shop spaces. A Staff ping when the pet event is up. A new dungeon announcement that ends by asking who wants to make a group.

The point is not to sound important. It is to make it easier for somebody else to do the thing. A reminder can be three words; an invitation can be one sentence and a tiny **"n.n"**. The room gets the information and a place to join in, without being asked to sit through a speech.

## PRETTY VIDEOGAME MAN CAUSED AN RPG EVENT

On November 29, 2023, Ame said he fell in love with RPGs because of **"un blade dancer muy lindo en lineage 2"** — a very handsome Blade Dancer in Lineage 2. Apparently aesthetics got a vote in the gaming pipeline.

## SONGS WITH A LOT OF LOVE

During an art-exhibit QOTD, Ame posted his own YouTube channel and said it contained some of his songs. He worried they might not entertain people, then described them as made **"with a lot of love"**. The hesitation and the attachment sit together: he was not loudly selling himself, but he was not pretending not to care either.

## PETTY CRIMES

- **"buena semana a tod@s ❤️"** — the warm greeting arrives attached to an actual useful offer.
- **Pretty videogame man caused an RPG event.** A very handsome Blade Dancer in Lineage 2 apparently helped recruit Ame into the genre. Mechanics may submit an appeal.
- **"with a lot of love".** Worries the songs may not entertain people; still tells the room exactly what went into making them.
- **Two partner-shop spaces became public infrastructure.** Spare capacity exists; Ame asks whether somebody needs it. Hoarding apparently failed the vibe check.
