# Ten

Ten is current **Staff**, but the useful Ten file is not a résumé. It is a collection of moments where somebody else supplies a social premise and Ten is already inside it before the room has finished explaining itself.

## Role progression

The August 2020 Lobby gives the cleanest entry point: Ten arrives with **`*barks*`**, Gilli handles access, Mugen answers **`Woof`**, and Anayss eventually provides the conventional `Welcome!!`. The `Role assigned!` line never names a role, so it stays onboarding rather than appointment chronology. Current Staff placement comes from current canon, not exporter archaeology.

## Running Gag — Screenshot Court accidentally acquires an heir

August 27 is the piece that makes the later `legacy` joke a pattern instead of a one-off. Ten realizes, **`i keep forgetting y'all record these`**. About thirty-five seconds later, the embarrassment has already been converted into **`at least i'll leave some kind of legacy i guess`**.

Two weeks later he returns to the same metaphor: **`i'm leaving a legacy here too apparently. i only hope my heir lives up to this.`** Mugen and Anthos `Hehe` the performance.

The timing is the whole mechanism. Ten does not deny that the room has receipts or spend ten messages trying to regain dignity. He notices the surveillance, gives it approximately half a minute, and decides the evidence now belongs to posterity. `At least` and `i guess` keep puncturing the grandeur even while he is constructing it. Screenshot Court has somehow become estate planning.

## Relationships

**Anthos.** Ten asks whether he can simply be `anthos apprentice like in game` when Tumbleweed paperwork demands a label, then later gets a `Hehe` from Anthos on the legacy/heir performance. The first phrase preserves game-context familiarity; it is not a formal UL apprenticeship.

**Rooks.** When Ten cannot decide on Tumbleweed branding, he openly submits `head empty rn`, borrows `whatever rooks said`, and accepts Rooks's joking adoption offer with almost no negotiation. Their surviving chemistry is comfortable premise-sharing, not literal family.

**Mugen.** Early bark-back recognition and later reciprocal `eli kiss` commands show the same thing in a different register: offered silliness gets returned in kind. Affection is real inside the scene; romance is not inferred.

**Janghoon / CA.** The April 2021 Wall return is mostly narrated by Janghoon: `portal to 2020??`, `an oldie`, `welcome back`. Ten answers only `😭`. Recognition carries the scene before Ten needs to explain himself.

## Episodes

**The Snitch Gets a Hammer.** September 24: `a snitch u say` → `*gets hammer*`; later, `if they die they die`; the burst eventually collapses back into `owo`. Mock-danger house play, not a real threat or enforcement function.

**Tumbleweed Branding Department Is Offline.** September 29: `anthos apprentice like in game`, `cos head empty rn`, `or whatever rooks said`, then `the anomaly vibe`. Anthos asks for taxonomy; Ten submits vibes and borrowed paperwork.

## Petty Crimes

**Forgot the Surveillance, Founded a Dynasty Anyway.** Thirty-five seconds from `y'all record these` to `legacy`; two weeks later there is apparently an heir.

**Head Empty, Borrowed Branding.** If naming the role is difficult, submit somebody else's answer until the vibe improves.

**Arrival by Bark.** The front door receives one canine stage direction and somehow the house understands.

Ten's through-line is not passivity. It is **low-friction participation**. He does not need first authorship to make a scene feel jointly made, and even being caught on camera can become another premise he accepts, bends, and hands back funnier.
