# Baby Lyssa / Ghoulie

Ghoulie's Wall mythology is loud enough that it can hide the other half of the file: in spring 2021 she was also treating **Daycare** like home turf.

March 29 is the cleanest statement. Baby Lyssa jokes that she basically owns that channel more than her own, tells Moon that **`if you chill in daycare enough you'd understand`**, and ranks the rooms:

**`daycare >>>> main vc >>> officers`**

That is not a permissions audit. It is better evidence than a permissions audit for the thing the wiki actually cares about: where somebody behaves like they live. Ghoulie is not announcing an admin role. She is talking like a tenant who has already moved the furniture and is now explaining neighborhood culture to visitors.

The joke becomes behavior on June 2. In the Wall, xanthos answers Gilli with **`my work here is done`**. Baby Lyssa immediately objects:

**`NAH GET YA ASS`**

xanthos answers that he already has his ass. Ghoulie drops the **Daycare channel tag**, then:

**`PLEASE`**

**`PLEASEEEEE`**

**`IM PEEING`**

The surviving chain proves the redirect. It does not prove xanthos obeyed. That distinction is almost funnier: Ghoulie is standing in one room trying to physically summon a man into the room she has decided is hers, and the archive refuses to give her enforcement powers she did not actually have.

This sits neatly beside the better-known Wall property saga. Ghoulie eventually calls the Wall **`my wall`**; other people joke that she lives there, Mugs asks for rent, Tae says she lives there rent-free, and Ren later frames her return as coming back to claim it. Daycare and the Wall are not the same bit, but together they show the same habit: Ghoulie turns channels into places she can socially occupy, claim, flee to, or drag somebody else toward.

The March 13 prehistory stays messier on purpose. Rooks posts an image the archive has not visually inspected. Mugen calls it **`Saturday morning at the UL strip club feat. @Baby Lyssa`**. Ghoulie does not reject the ridiculous workplace premise; she objects to procedure: **`y’all supposed to be bullying the customers not the stripper`**. Hours later ShiyaX is still asking whether she is joining. The contradiction is useful without being "fixed": the paperwork is socially fuzzy while Ghoulie is already fluent enough to file a labor grievance against an imaginary strip club.

That is the recurring character move. Ghoulie does not need formal ownership to make a room feel claimed. She just starts behaving as if the lease is emotionally obvious.
