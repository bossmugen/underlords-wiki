# Amexistir

Amexistir arrived speaking Spanish, and Ren answered in kind, calling him **Ame** and handling the check-in without making his limited English a spectacle. When he returned in late 2023, Anayss described a simpler plan: somewhere chill to spend time with Kassandtina. The officers noticed his usefulness, but Snow wanted to give him room before assigning responsibilities.
