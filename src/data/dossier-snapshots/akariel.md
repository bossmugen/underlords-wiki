# Akariel

Akariel asked for a place on the Wall before anybody had prepared a charge. Ren tried to make admission sound like a tribunal — `You got to e a r n your place here` — and Akariel accepted the terms with `fair lmaooo`. No appeal, no dignity-saving maneuver. They had applied to the prosecution and were apparently happy with the onboarding packet.

## The Wall is a group project

Akariel does not only get filed; they help keep the Wall supplied. A word-of-the-day post becomes `ass`, which gives Tofu a runway. In a separate exchange, Tofu complains that Akariel is taking everybody's souls and asks for the smallest possible policy change:

> `how about you not take our souls at all *Satan*`

Akariel considers the proposal and answers:

> `naaah`

The missing first premise stays missing. The part that survives is the ease of the exchange: Tofu can accuse, Akariel can decline in five letters, and neither needs to stop the bit to explain the paperwork.

## A dance card, then another event

Qin-Mu's WALTZ caption names `Yoza and @Akariel`. That puts Akariel on Yoza's staged dance card, in the middle of UL's habit of recruiting people into themed nonsense. Akariel later posts an image during the DUSK TIL DAWN submission window. The submissions are part of the story; the uninspected pictures do not get invented captions.

Voice Challenge gives Akariel another way into the room's creative machinery. They choose `Magnetic Moon` by Tiffany Young for their character presentation, then ask Han for help editing the submission. A good song choice and a practical request for help can coexist without anybody needing to turn either into a personality diagnosis.
