# Akariel / Zyrcant

Akariel was Zyrcant in Dragon Raja, and didn't make a production out of the distinction. Asked for a club at the door, they offered an old Amaurot connection with a shrug about whether the club still existed. By summer, the name was turning up in far livelier places: beside Yoza in WALTZ, among the winners of UL's second Kahoot, and under ShiyaX's accusation of being **caught in 4k**.
