# Akuma / Matsu

The same person surfaces under three eras of names. In the 2020 Lobby form, **Sebno** identifies with Underlords and mentions Rebellation. In November 2021, **神Nyx道** lists Abaddon. By the August 2026 reunion, **Akuma / Matsu** walks back into Whiskey. The stable account joins those records; no guesswork about matching usernames is necessary.

## People who remembered

Anthos answered the December 2020 intake with **`haiii seb!!!`**. In 2021 Mugen's **`WEBA bb sebs!`** carried the old nickname across the new display name. When Akuma / Matsu returned in 2026, Mugen's first instinct was not to ask for identification but to blame food: **`Of course food would summon u`**. The joke lands because the recognition is already there.

## Episodes

**The Sebno check-in — December 2020.** A self-authored form places Sebno in the old Underlords/Rebellation wording, followed by Anthos's immediate greeting.

**The Abaddon-era return — November 2021.** The club field changes, but Mugen still calls the account *sebs*. That continuity belongs to the people, not to a role array.

**Food summons Akuma — August 2026.** The reunion greeting opens into a present-day college update: IT, music technology, production and DJ ambitions, guitar, a cousin teaching keys, and a plan to use the school's music lab. It is a practical dream with an equipment strategy, not merely an old username making a cameo.

Mugen's **`Of course food would summon u`** is a reunion callback and a character-specific welcome, not proof of an established universal food nickname. **`WEBA bb sebs!`** is the clearest older recognition line.
