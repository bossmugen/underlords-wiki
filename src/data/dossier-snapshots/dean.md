Dean's UL biography starts before UL does. He was already part of Mugen's older online-family circle from another game, alongside Key, Zepp, Kal, New, and GenGrey, roughly a year before Dragon Raja. Within that older social world Dean was Mugen's online son and called her `mum`. That matters because the later Discord archive does not create the relationship. It catches an existing relationship being carried into a new house.

## 2020 — an older family relationship enters UL's paperwork

The first especially useful UL receipt is September 29, 2020, during the update of `Underlords_Tumbleweed.png`. Anthos writes `lets add dean to mugs then from there we can add cel and woo etc`, and later makes the task explicit: `we r updating the UL family tumbleweed`.

Read chronologically, the line is not Dean's recruitment scene. The social edge already existed. What UL is doing is documenting it in the group's increasingly deranged family-map grammar. Dean is being placed next to Mugen because the mother/son relationship came with them from before Raja, not because somebody invented a new kinship bit on September 29.

A month later, on October 22, Mugen posts an attachment to the Wall and follows it with `Dean ain't playin no games 👀`. The attachment remains undescribed, so the page does not make Dean the subject, maker, or victor of an image it has not actually inspected. What survives safely is simpler: Dean is already a named person in Mugen's ordinary Wall commentary. He is not entering as a mysterious late addition who needs an introduction every time his name appears.

## 2021 — Beansprout, exposure, and the family language becoming visible

By March 2021 Dean is speaking in the Wall himself. On March 23 he asks, `Is this where you expose Peopl`, then follows with `Ehehehhehe`. The timing is almost a miniature onboarding into UL evidence culture: identify the room's function, then immediately sound delighted about the possibility. It does not prove Dean became some designated screenshot hunter. It does show that the Wall's social purpose is legible to him, and that his first preserved reaction to that purpose is not fear so much as mischief.

Six days later the archive resolves the account cleanly. Gilli asks `WHOM IS BEANSPROUT`, then `DEAN?`; the account `supporttech900`, displayed as `✔️ Support Tech`, answers `YES`. Later that night Mugen calls the same account `My little deansprout uwu🌱`. The nickname chain is useful because it is relational rather than administrative. `Support Tech` looks official if ripped away from the conversation, but the archive does not earn a technical appointment. `Beansprout` and `deansprout`, by contrast, are clearly part of how people are talking to and about Dean inside the room.

April shows the family-map layer spreading sideways. On April 19 Ren posts that `Dean was watching scary stuff last night` and adds `I did him a spook`. Four days later Ren calls Dean `my bro`; Dean, apparently less invested in keeping the fake-family filing cabinet straight, asks `What are you again RV`. The asymmetry is funny because Ren is supplying the relationship label while Dean is checking the chart. It supports an easy teasing familiarity between them without needing to inflate that scene into a ranked friendship or overwrite the much older Mugen–Dean mother/son relationship.

The Wall catches Dean on the receiving end again in June. On June 3 his protest is `WhY U expOse Me`. The warped capitalization makes the complaint performative rather than bureaucratic; he is not filing an access-control ticket about the Wall, he is participating in the same exposure grammar he had recognized in March. On June 26 the archive then gets one of the cleanest continuity receipts on the page. Dean writes `Only if pizza man was hot`, then turns to Mugen: `Don't you agree mum`.

That `mum` is small and decisive. No family-tree chart is needed in that moment. Dean simply addresses Mugen the way he already did in their older online-family relationship. The old bond has become ordinary enough inside UL that it can sit in the middle of a pizza-man joke without explanation.

By July, even peer characterization starts to preserve Dean's comic presence. Woohyuk writes `lmao dean cracks me up`. The surrounding attachment context is incomplete, so the site does not pretend to know the precise joke. The useful point is the social position: Dean is not merely a name on Mugen's old-family branch. Other people in UL are reacting to him as an active source of amusement in their own right.

## 2022 — the name no longer needs setup

The 2022 material is thinner, which is itself useful. On September 3 Gabu addresses `Dean bb` in a joke contrasting Wolfie as a giant with Gilli as an ant. Roughly an hour later Mugen writes that `Dean saw purple and he thought purple = wolfie`.

Those lines are not one reconstructed exchange and the unseen prompt stays unseen. But by this point nobody is explaining who Dean is, why he is there, or what `Dean bb` means. The archive has moved from arrival and relationship-definition into ordinary household use. Dean can simply be the person who makes the wrong purple association and gets lovingly filed for it.

## 2023 — `Mug's son` survives the game that introduced UL

A recovered April 3, 2023 Whiskey screenshot makes the long arc unusually visible. Dean's server display reads `Dean (Mug's son)`.

The phrasing is almost comically explicit, but its importance is chronological. The mother/son relationship predates Dragon Raja. UL's 2020 Tumbleweed records it. Dean calls Mugen `mum` in 2021. Three years into UL, the same social relationship is still visible in his server identity. That is continuity, not a new title and not biological kinship.

The same screenshot also catches Dean fixating on a `999` unread-style count and hunting for the red notification dots. His own wording is `my OCD my ADHD whatever I can't stand It`. The site preserves the line because it explains the immediate behavior in the scene: he cannot leave the unread indicators alone. It does **not** turn casual self-description into a diagnosis. What the scene safely adds to the biography is a very concrete little frustration — Dean repeatedly checking the interface because the notification clutter is driving him up the wall.

Across the surviving record, Dean's progression is therefore unusually coherent. He does not begin as a UL role and later acquire relationships. He begins with a relationship older than UL, then the new community learns to carry it. The Tumbleweed formalizes the social edge in 2020; the Wall gives him his own Beansprout voice and exposure reactions in 2021; by 2022 he is ordinary enough to be teased without introduction; by 2023 `Mug's son` is still sitting in plain sight.

Permanent Platelet is the roster label. The stronger biography is the portability underneath it: one online family relationship survives a game change, gets absorbed into UL's own stupid family paperwork, and remains socially legible years later without needing to be reinvented.
