# Nemo

Nemo — also known as **Namo** and **Cinnamoroll** — is an active UL Sniper. His place in the group is easiest to understand through what he actually did: rather than only waiting for applicants to come to UL, he became known as a traveling recruiter who moved through other clubs, made friends, and sometimes brought those relationships back into Underlords.

The exact appointment chronology is not preserved cleanly enough to date his Sniper role from the surviving exports, so the bio does not force one. What is clear is the shape of the job. Nemo's recruitment depended less on standing at a door and more on being socially mobile enough that other communities became reachable through friendship.

## 2023 — Nemo in his own words

The strongest direct voice material arrives in QOTD in early 2023. A deleted Discord account that had been difficult to identify becomes much less mysterious because the person repeatedly names himself. On February 2 he writes **`nemo too😎`** and **`invader nemo`**. On February 21, answering a Mafia character-book prompt, he starts with **`Name: Nemo`**.

That same answer gives the cleaner personality texture: **`Weapon: no weapon bish you gonna get these fins`**. He describes himself as weird and loud at times, and the fish/shark vocabulary keeps recurring around him. The joke works because Nemo participates in it himself; the room is not assigning an identity he never uses.

By March 28, Gabu has converted the name into an assignment and jokes that Nemo's first mission for UL is simply **`Find nemo`**. By this point the Sniper title, the traveling-recruiter reputation, and the fish joke all sit in the same social picture: somebody associated with movement between groups, now being turned into his own navigation problem.

The deleted-account resolution matters for the biography because it restores continuity to those 2023 scenes. It does not solve every `Deleted User` in every older export. This specific QOTD account is Nemo; unrelated deleted placeholders remain separate until independently identified.

## 2026 — An older profile comes back into view

During the August 2026 reunion, Mugen resurfaced an older Biodata screenshot under the name **Cinnamoroll**. The identity bridge is locked: Cinnamoroll is Nemo. The card itself is historical, so 2026 is the date it re-entered the archive, not the date every detail on it became true.

The profile adds a quieter layer that the role history cannot. Nemo describes a strong Sanrio interest, especially **Badtz-Maru and Cinnamoroll**; mentions a husky named **Leo**; lists **baking and snacks**; and says he was **learning Mandarin Chinese**. Those details are not used to explain why he recruited well, and they do not need to. They simply recover more of the person who had been flattened into a role and a deleted username.

Taken in sequence, Nemo's biography becomes more complete over time. The role history remembers somebody who moved between clubs and built bridges through friendship. The 2023 QOTD material restores his own voice — loud, fish-brained, self-mythologizing. The older Biodata card, resurfacing years later, fills in domestic and personal details that the operational record never would have preserved.

That is the progression that matters: **from function, to voice, to fuller personhood**. Nemo is not interesting because every part of him explains the Sniper job. He is interesting because the archive slowly stops reducing him to it.
