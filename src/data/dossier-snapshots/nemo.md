# Nemo

Nemo — also known as **Namo**, **Sushi**, and **Cinnamoroll** — is an active UL Sniper, but the useful biography begins before the title. The archive catches somebody who is socially mobile across Dragon Raja clubs, responsive to being shown around, comfortable enough to notice aloud when he is being absorbed into a new group, and later willing to make his own name into a running fish joke.

The exact Sniper appointment chronology is not preserved cleanly enough to date from surviving exports. The traveling-recruiter behavior is nevertheless user-confirmed: Nemo visited other clubs, made friends, and sometimes brought people back toward UL. Friendship is the important mechanism. The evidence does not support recasting that social movement as espionage, intelligence work, or a separate command title.

## 2020 — getting shown around, then noticing the absorption

In an October 2020 conversation, Nemo jokes that several groups had recruited him almost immediately after he woke up, then says the experience was actually nice because people showed him around. In the same period he talks about switching servers before the larger transfer context. When Mugen explains that she had hopped servers and Underlords followed her, Nemo calls it pretty cool and says it is nice not everyone from the old group quit.

The clearest line comes when Nemo says **`I know I'm becoming one of you`** and Mugen answers **`absorbbbb`**. It is joke language, but it makes the social process visible: Nemo notices belonging happening while it is happening and joins the bit rather than waiting for somebody else to narrate him into the room.

## 2022 — Sushi is still Nemo

The stable account later appears under **Sushi**. In June 2022, Sushi asks whether anyone has watched Naruto. Rummy answers with mock disbelief that anybody in the room could have missed it; Sushi replies with laughter and `Idk`. The scene is small and useful. It gives the middle years ordinary fandom chatter instead of forcing the biography to fill them with generic Sniper labor.

## 2023 — Nemo in his own words

The strongest direct voice material arrives in QOTD. A deleted Discord account that had been difficult to identify becomes much less mysterious because the person repeatedly names himself. On February 2 he writes **`nemo too😎`** and **`invader nemo`**. On February 21, answering a Mafia character-book prompt, he starts with **`Name: Nemo`**.

That same answer gives the cleaner personality texture: **`Weapon: no weapon bish you gonna get these fins`**. He describes himself as weird and loud at times, and the fish/shark vocabulary keeps recurring around him. The joke works because Nemo participates in it himself; the room is not assigning an identity he never uses.

Other QOTD answers broaden the voice. A pirate-crime prompt gets the unusually formal **`Illegal possession and smuggling of arms/weaponry`**. An intro-music prompt gets **`In my head by queens of the stone age while driving somewhere with friends`** — not just a song, but a little social scene. In a Sanrio exchange Nemo says friends have called him Cinnamoroll, so he will choose Cinnamoroll, then keeps guessing as the room corrects him. Those are individual receipts, not a personality test, but together they make his communication style more visible: often brief, concrete, and willing to give the prompt one more odd turn.

By March 28, Gabu has converted the name into an assignment and jokes that Nemo's first mission for UL is simply **`Find nemo`**. By this point the Sniper title, traveling-recruiter reputation, and fish joke can sit in the same social picture without pretending they are the same thing.

The deleted-account resolution matters because it restores continuity to those QOTD scenes. It does not solve every `Deleted User` in every older export. This specific QOTD account is Nemo; unrelated deleted placeholders remain separate until independently identified.

## 2026 — an older profile comes back into view

During the August 2026 reunion, Mugen resurfaced an older Biodata screenshot under the name **Cinnamoroll**. The identity bridge is locked: Cinnamoroll is Nemo. The card itself is historical, so 2026 is the date it re-entered the archive, not the date every detail on it became current.

The profile adds a quieter layer the role history cannot. Nemo describes a strong Sanrio interest, especially **Badtz-Maru and Cinnamoroll**; mentions a husky named **Leo**; lists **baking and snacks**; and says he was **learning Mandarin Chinese**. Those details are not used to explain why he recruited well. They simply recover more of the person who had been flattened into a role and a deleted username.

Taken in sequence, Nemo becomes clearer without becoming over-explained. The early archive shows social movement and a person noticing his own assimilation into the house. The middle preserves ordinary fandom chat. The QOTD material restores his compact, ridiculous voice. The resurfaced Biodata returns domestic and personal details the operational record had no reason to keep.

That is the progression that matters: **from function, to voice, to fuller personhood**. Nemo is not interesting because every part of him explains the Sniper job. He is interesting because the archive gradually stops reducing him to it.
