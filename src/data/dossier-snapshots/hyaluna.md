# HyaLuna

HyaLuna is one of the people who belongs to Underlords before the later org chart can explain her. Mugen founded UL in March 2020, and HyaLuna was one of the four Altissa friends who came with her, alongside Ritha, WolfPhenix, and Suzimasu.

The early private-room story has two different doors. `🍆ulxxx` was the tiny discussion chamber for Mugen, Snow, and HyaLuna, with Gilli added later. Luna did not request that room. The personal room she actually asked for was `🍑lunas-house-of-kitties`.

House of Kitties gives the file its sharpest little character beat. The scene-locally resolved Luna account opens with **`this is mine`**. Snow later gives the room a Luna-controlled keycode and its own music-bot setup. Apparently the correct response to four words of territorial confidence was to install utilities.

Three days later the same old trust appears in a more serious setting. When Mugen brings the ScarletMoon offer into Bosses Office, she tells the wider leadership that she and HyaLuna discussed it the night before, then opens the question to everybody else for pros and cons.

Later roster canon records HyaLuna as a full Officer and historical Underboss / Deputy. That title belongs to her later command history; the early rooms already show why she was close to the center without needing to turn room access into a rank ladder.

HyaLuna also carries the public **Mugen's Concubines** tag. Founder, private-room regular, later Underboss, concubine. UL contained multitudes and none of them were dignified.

This is the original HyaLuna. She is distinct from Candy and other Luna-shaped identities.
