# Miihi

## The person who answers `evidence`

Miihi did not need a long briefing when the Wall got stupid. Anayss could summon her with a name and the word `evidence`, and she would turn up ready to recognize the mess. Baby Lyssa's complaint about being beaten to it gives the moment its punchline: the room knew Miihi could move fast.

## Three rounds, same familiar face

Her October 2020 Lobby welcome was ordinary enough: MEE6 did the automated greeting, Gilli said hello and asked for her IGN, club, and age. By the following Photoshoot season Miihi was returning for **LOVE**, **Lonely**, and **Flowers**. She wasn't only a Wall evidence regular; she was also somebody who kept coming back to submit things when the creative rounds opened.
