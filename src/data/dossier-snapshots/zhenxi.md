# Zhēnxī

Zhēnxī keeps the circle small. The important part is not that they lack friends; they say they have plenty. The distinction is between knowing people and letting a few of them become genuinely close. Trust and respect matter more than a large contact list.

## The front door and the Wall share a timetable

On May 21, 2021, the Underlords Stripclub Bouncer posted the Lobby welcome. Ren checked the name, called Zhēnxī 'dear', and pointed them toward the Discord tour. Zhēnxī answered with 'I am scare'. Ren's 'Don’t get lost now UwU' is the tiny hand on the shoulder before the tour begins.

The tour did not get much of a head start. At 23:40:30 Ren tagged Zhēnxī in the Wall. Six seconds later came 'NOOOOOOOOOOO', followed by 'NOT THE WALL OF SHAME FAME'. Ansun called it 'shamed within 6 minutes of entry'; Ren asked 'New record?'. The visible welcome-to-tag interval is about 6m53s. The room did not need a formal welcome speech to explain what the Wall was.

## Compliments have a clearance level

Zhēnxī's preferred delivery system is 'insult people politely'. They say they have many friends but only a few deep bonds; when asked about compliments, they make the rule explicit: praise is for a 'really close friend'.

Then they name Rummy.

'You're amazing, though. Love you' gets a direct, all-caps answer back: 'ZHEEENNN I LOVEE YOU TOOOO'. In another QOTD exchange, Rummy offers 'I’ll be one irl too' after Zhēnxī says they want friends IRL. The affection is not hidden behind a solemn announcement. It arrives inside the ordinary back-and-forth, and both people let it be heard.

## The Wall is not a one-way street

In July 2022, Zhēnxī posts a screenshot to the Wall, tags Ren with 'so uh-', laughs at Ren's 'STAWP', then calls the filing 'Deserving'. Gabu adds a hydration roast; Zhēnxī backs her. The person who once panicked at being tagged has learned the more useful rule: the Wall is not just somewhere you get sent. It is also something you can do to your friends.

## Relationships

- **Rummy:** Explicitly named as a 'really close friend'; their affection is direct and reciprocal. Rummy also volunteers to be Zhēnxī's IRL friend, without that exchange proving an in-person meeting.
- **Ren:** Gives the Lobby tour, tags Zhēnxī in the Wall, then becomes a later target of Zhēnxī's own Wall filing.
- **Gabu:** Joins the July 2022 Wall exchange with a hydration roast; Zhēnxī backs her.

## Episodes

- **The Tour Had Barely Started. The Wall Already Had a File.** — May 21, 2021. Lobby intake, Ren's tour handoff, and the first Wall panic.
- **The Wall Is Not a One-Way Street.** — July 7, 2022. Zhēnxī returns to the Wall as a poster and calls the result 'Deserving'.

## Running Gags / recurring voice

- **Polite insults:** compliments are rare and deliberate, not absent.
- **Wall fluency:** can panic at a filing and later use the same machinery against a friend.
- **All-caps affection:** Rummy gets the close-friend exception in a very loud font.
