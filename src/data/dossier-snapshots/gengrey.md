## BEFORE THE SERVER HAS A CAMERA

GenGrey's relationship to Mugen starts before the surviving Underlords Discord file can see it. Mugs confirms that GenGrey and Key already knew Mugen through another game before Dragon Raja and before UL. Dean, Zepp, and Kal also belong to that older orbit, but the archive does not flatten them into one identical friendship clock or one closeness ranking.

That older history changes how the later Discord receipts should be read. GenGrey does not enter the story as a random Raja stranger whose importance begins when she crosses Lobby. Discord is arriving late to a relationship that already exists.

Mugs also confirms that GenGrey is a tattoo artist. That is useful real-life context because it tells us something concrete about the person outside UL. It does **not** give the wiki permission to invent a studio, city, style, clientele, training history, schedule, or personality. The username `greyinked` may look wonderfully convenient beside the confirmed profession; it is not the evidence for the profession.

## SHE SIGNS THE DOOR HERSELF

April 7, 2020. Lobby. The stable `greyinked` account gives the room one clean line:

> `Hi - GenGrey`

For identity work, this is excellent. The author ID, username, nickname, and self-identification all converge. For personality work, it is deliberately small. One greeting cannot prove that GenGrey is terse, shy, cool, private, blunt, or anything else a biography might be tempted to staple onto three words.

The surrounding Lobby sequence is onboarding-shaped: Snow had asked people to introduce themselves, and Mugen is dealing with role-assignment permissions seconds later. None of that makes April 7 GenGrey's first meeting with Mugen, her Staff appointment, or the start of the older friendship. It is the first clean point where the surviving public Discord record can confidently say: **this account is GenGrey.**

## THE NAME SURVIVES MORE CLEANLY THAN THE VOICE

Later 2020 material gives traces rather than a dense authored character run. In September, Mugen posts a numbered grouping that includes `#2 - gengrey, yummibears`. The recovered context does not tell us what `#2` means. It therefore remains a number without an invented team, rank, event assignment, or relationship theory attached to it.

The wider Slice index also confirms a small GenGrey footprint and one direct attachment. That attachment has not been safely identified and visually inspected here, so it stays undescribed. Even if the media is later recovered, posted-by will still need to remain separate from made-by, photographed-by, tattooed-by, or featuring. GenGrey's real tattoo-art profession makes visual overreach especially tempting; provenance still wins.

What this file **does not** currently give is just as important. It does not safely establish GenGrey's recurring humor style, favorite fandoms, conflict behavior, embarrassment pattern, ordinary speech rhythm, or the things friends repeatedly tease her about. Those are person-first questions worth continuing to mine. Their present absence is an archive boundary, not a personality diagnosis.

## AN OLDER FRIENDSHIP, THEN A LATER BRIDGE

New belongs to a different layer of the social map. New is GenGrey's friend, but New meets Mugen later through that broader connection. GenGrey's older friendship with Mugen and Key therefore cannot be copied wholesale onto New's timeline.

That makes GenGrey socially important without inventing an Underlords recruiting office for her. She connects parts of Mugen's online life in the ordinary human sense: an older friendship exists, and a later friend eventually crosses into the wider network through that relationship. The archive does not currently show GenGrey processing New through Lobby, assigning roles, or making a formal referral.

## CURRENT BOUNDARY

GenGrey is currently filed under Staff. An old Lobby export shows a VIP role on her message, but export-time role arrays do not date appointments and do not override the living roster. The clean evidence used here does not recover a Staff promotion date or enough duty-specific material to reverse-engineer one.

So the current public file is intentionally asymmetrical. We know GenGrey is a woman, tattoo artist, older online friend of Mugen and Key, friend of New, the person behind `greyinked`, and current Staff. We know the relationship history begins before UL. We do **not** yet know enough from the surviving authored text to manufacture a full temperament, hobby catalog, relationship hierarchy, or dramatic development arc.

That is the right place to leave it until the archive earns more. `Hi - GenGrey` looks like an introduction if you only read Discord. In the actual chronology, it is documentation finally catching up.
