# GenGrey

GenGrey is current **Staff**, a tattoo artist in the locked biographical record, and — much more important for understanding where he enters this story — part of Mugen's older online-family circle with Dean, Key, Zepp, Kal, and New. That circle predates Dragon Raja and Underlords by roughly a year. So when Discord finally catches GenGrey in April 2020, the archive is not recording two strangers meeting. It is recording an older relationship finally walking through a newer front door.

The clean surviving line is almost comically small:

> `Hi - GenGrey`

That is his April 7, 2020 Lobby introduction from the stable `greyinked` account. It is excellent identity evidence. It is nearly useless personality evidence. The distinction is exactly why this dossier stays smaller than the loud files around it.

There are later traces, but not yet a responsible second act. In September 2020 Mugen posts an unexplained numbered grouping that includes `#2 - gengrey, yummibears`. Nearby messages get us closer to the moment but still never tell us what the numbers mean. Team? Room? Event? Assignment? The answer is currently: the archive has not earned one. Turning `#2` into a duo, ranking, job, or relationship would be the kind of tidy lie this wiki is specifically trying not to tell.

Slice gives the stable account a tiny indexed footprint — two messages and one direct attachment — but the underlying text and media were not recovered cleanly enough in the current source package to describe. One unresolved image does not become a personality because the page has empty space.

That leaves GenGrey with a deliberately unusual biography shape. The most important context is relational and pre-archival: he belongs to the online family that knew Mugen before UL had a name. The strongest surviving Discord receipt is simply him identifying himself. Current Staff status belongs to the present roster; an old export-time VIP role does not tell us when anything changed.

A mature wiki should be able to say **we know this person matters more than the surviving scene count currently explains**. GenGrey is one of those cases. The page therefore preserves the old-family provenance, the exact self-identification, and the unresolved hooks without inventing a charming archetype to make the card feel fuller. If later mining recovers his two Slice messages or decodes the September grouping, the file can grow. Until then, one exact scene is better than four fake ones.
