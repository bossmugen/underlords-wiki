# BishopThaGuru

## Cast

BishopThaGuru's July 2021 Waltz appearance has a little story built into the caption: teaching Tofu to dance turned out cuter than expected. Not a grand declaration, not a dance-floor rivalry. Just somebody arriving at a two-person Photoshoot prompt and deciding the lesson itself was the funny, sweet part.

## Relationships

**Tofu** had been giving Bishop verdicts long before the Waltz picture. When she declared that anything involving him would end up on the Wall of Shame, he went straight into mock interrogation: who had she talked to, was she with the feds? Was her prediction a compliment or a warning? `Both.` Three weeks later he picked the same deadpan judge for a Waltz lesson and called the result unexpectedly cute. The teasing was already a two-person routine; the dance gave it another setting.
