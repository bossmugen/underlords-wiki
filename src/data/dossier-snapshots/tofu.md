# Tofu

Tofu is current **Staff**. The bee is optional. The evidence bag is apparently not.

The recovered Discord record gives Tofu a server-join timestamp of June 20, 2021. Five days later she has the earliest Wall post found in the checked surviving Tofu interval. The attachment stays sealed; the date is chronology, not a license to hallucinate the image or promote a Discord join timestamp into an in-game admission date.

Three days after that, ShiyaX tells her **`you’re all over the wall`**. Tofu answers **`Shiya no I’m scared of the walls`**. ShiyaX: **`you better`**. It is a remarkably efficient first-week orientation: Tofu is already using the Wall and already understands that the Wall can turn around and use her.

The same June 25 date supplies a cleaner verbal receipt. Somebody mentions spending part of a rebate. Tofu immediately chants **`Wall of shame, wall of shame. Post the rebate 😝`**. Seven minutes later a pooled Deleted User observes **`Tryna expose everyone :0`**. Tofu answers: **`Maybe ^^`**.

By August 27, a direct reply to one of Tofu's Wall posts is simply **`OH GOD NO`**. Tofu answers **`HAHAHAHAHAHA`**. The underlying post remains uninterpreted. The social beat does not: before the later bee-spam prosecution makes reciprocal exposure explicit, the room is already greeting Tofu's Wall contributions like incoming weather.

The important complication arrives September 20, when the evidence system turns around. A screenshot appears. Tofu reacts **`PUTTING ME ON BLAST!`**. Thirty-seven seconds later: **`I'll fucking do it again too.`** The attachment itself was not visually inspected, so the wiki does not narrate what the exhibit shows. It does not need to. Tofu's two lines contain the entire contradiction: object to becoming evidence, then announce future recidivism.

That is why the recurring behavior is **reciprocal evidence culture**, not prosecutor identity. Tofu is not neatly divided into curator mode and defendant mode. Both are active. You can demand the receipt, become the receipt, complain about the receipt, and still endorse the underlying behavior before a minute has passed.

October turns Tofu back toward the prosecution table. Tofu posts **`I'm not saying @Cookies and Juicebox is a cochino but, he's a cochino.`** with a screenshot attachment. Cookie answers **`:PandaRun:`**. Three days later another screenshot exchange produces Cookie's **`its not how it looks like`**, **`i swear`**, another panda run, and **`so done`**. The binaries remain undescribed; the social mechanism is already visible without them. Tofu presents an exhibit. Cookie disputes the exhibit. The panda exits the jurisdiction.

A separate Tofu reflex survives in two 2021 scenes, and it is deliberately narrower. On September 16, ShiyaX asks **`theres a limit ?`**. Tofu uses a true Discord Reply: **`At least pace yourself`**. The image and exact activity behind the question remain unresolved. The useful thing is the grammatical move: the answer is not a blanket veto. It is a limit delivered from inside the premise.

December 5 repeats that structure. An uninspected Wall image prompts Tofu to say the object is supposedly difficult to remove, then eventually: **`Try em…just not on a body part you treasure`**. Ren's later response is essentially *why would she even try them*. Again, the wiki refuses to identify the object from context it does not have. What repeats is Tofu's communication move: the room is already entertaining the questionable idea, so Tofu adds a practical boundary instead of pretending the idea can be made nonexistent by disapproval.

This does not make Tofu UL's safety officer, clinician, harm-reduction specialist, or designated adult in the room. Authority is not what the archive is showing. It is showing limits delivered from inside the same unserious culture Tofu is helping document.

Then Slice opens 2022 with Tofu's **`NO ONE TOUCH ANYTHING NOW THAT WE'RE IN 2022.`** It is the first surviving line in the recovered 2022–2023 transcript, which makes it an excellent archive-boundary joke and terrible evidence of room control. No one should turn the luck of transcript survival into a moderation appointment.

By August 2024, the screenshot reflex is still alive. Tofu writes: **`This is isn't far off from a convo he and I had a few days ago... where is that screenshot`**. The identity of `he` is not safely resolved in this receipt. The verb is. Three years after the Wall was putting Tofu on blast, the question is still not *do I remember this?* It is **where is the screenshot**.

That continuity is enough. Request the receipt. Post the receipt. Become the receipt. Object loudly. Promise to repeat the underlying crime. Years later, ask where another receipt went. The Wall trained a generation badly, and Tofu kept the filing instinct.
