# LEXI

LEXI enters the clean record through paperwork and escapes it almost immediately.

On January 11, Gilli asks for **IGN, club, server, age**. Stable account `strawburrilexi` answers **Okaa-san, LoliParadise, S8**, followed by a historical age the public Wiki does not need. The first three fields are enough to pin down the scene without making the usual administrative hallucination: she is at UL's Discord door while explicitly presenting another club. That is presence, not an admission timestamp.

Gilli points her toward the Discord Tour. Minutes later, the earliest surfaced Whiskey media row is already LEXI speaking fluent reaction-GIF. Clipboard, map, anime reaction. Orientation appears to have lasted approximately one commercial break.

The more useful pattern is what she repeatedly aims at Staff afterward. Valentine's Day gets a greeting. March 2 gets a small craft disaster: LEXI says something she wrote with Japanese pens smudged because she forgot to let them dry, complains about her own mistake, then sends the good-day wish anyway. The attachment stays sealed, so the Wiki does not pretend to know what the page looked like. The behavior is already visible in the sentence: **imperfection does not cancel the gesture**.

The next morning she makes the point explicit: **`love you all be safe, know that you are loved and you are worth it.`** Repetition across separate dates turns this from random room niceness into a person-specific habit of using the public room to push care outward.

Then there is Meowk. Existing Wiki canon resolves that 2021 display account to **Alkey**, but the relationship reading does not depend on the bridge. February already has repeated `I love you` language in the dyad. On March 6 LEXI leaves with **`I have to go now I love you idiot`**; twenty seconds later comes **`I love you too shorty`**.

The nouns are insults. The sentence is not. `Idiot` and `shorty` are affectionate packaging around an explicit reciprocal handoff, and that is exactly where the Wiki stops. No romance upgrade, no exclusivity, no `Shorty` alias, no ranking of the relationship. Just two people whose house-language apparently requires tenderness to arrive wearing tiny boxing gloves.
