# Daya

Daya is a current Pit Boss and Recruiter, but the archive gets more interesting whenever those titles stop being the noun. Her chat voice is warm and fast: hearts in Lobby, doubled exclamation points, casual lowercase, practical next steps. She can tell somebody that real life comes before game dailies, remember that a returner does not need to redo the rules, collect a Gilli song for Spotify, push communal doodles toward the official Raja poster, and then meet a Wall prosecution with **`i am shameless 😎`** followed by **`i have no rebuttal`**. Responsibility never seems to require a separate ceremonial Daya voice.

## 2021–2022 — CHECKPOINT, PROMOTION, THEN THE FRONT DOOR

The earliest clean Lobby checkpoint recovered in this pass is December 31, 2021, when the stable Dayadream account posts **`S13/Dayadream/22`** and Mugen later supplies the welcome/role packet. That dates Daya in the front-door record; it does not prove this was her first contact with UL.

Mugen announced Daya as the newest Underlords Officer on May 14, 2022. A month later, Slice gives a better measure of the person behind the promotion. Somebody is trying to bend college around Dragon Raja dailies; Daya answers: **`dont stress. prioritize school, real life always first`**. It is not a policy authorship claim. It is a clean statement of priority in the moment: the game is allowed to lose.

July catches a different register. The Wall gets Daya to **`i am shameless 😎`** and, three seconds later, **`i have no rebuttal`**. The sunglasses turn surrender into the bit. The archive does not need to pretend Daya never feels embarrassment; it shows that in this scene she can be caught, concede the case, and make the concession funny.

That same practical looseness becomes visible in Lobby by August. When a newcomer's role assignment is still pending, Daya does not make them wait uselessly. She sends the server map, Biodata, and Whiskey Room immediately, explains what still needs to happen, and tells them another officer will finish the remaining role step. Six days later Fang gets the same treatment: wait a minute for roles, but use the minute to read the rules, find the map, and introduce yourself. Later that month Daya is also posting **`role assigned!`** herself.

On August 27 she recognizes a returner with **`hii again!`**, tells them they do not need to view the rules again, and asks for a moment to get them set up. The machine may see an arrival event. Daya sees somebody who has already done part of this before. That is a tiny but useful distinction: procedure is there to orient the person, not make the person reenact procedure for its own sake.

By November, her welcome routine has expanded into a safety brief. She repeatedly explains the Platelet/minor color cue, asks people to use caution, directs controversial or sensitive issues away from public escalation, and then gives the practical map through Biodata, the server index, and Whiskey. On November 20 the packet begins **`its good to have u!!`**. The rules are shared house rules, not all authored by Daya. Her character evidence is in the delivery: welcome first, then make the boundary legible before the newest person can accidentally step over it.

## 2023 — MUSIC, POSTERS, AND SCREENSHOT JAIL

March gets Daya out of the front-desk frame completely. After another Gilli music pick lands, Daya says **`@Gilli another slay`** and **`u giving me more songs for my spotify`**. It is a small ordinary-life receipt: she is not merely acknowledging the song; she is keeping music she likes. The archive does not tell us her genres or justify a special intimacy ranking with Gilli. It does tell us Spotify is one of the places UL music escapes to when Daya wants to keep it.

Then the Wall starts eating the filing system. On April 2 Daya correctly identifies HicUUOOOOGH as Kiro. Kiro answers **`Who's kiro`**. Daya, faced with aliases turning basic identity resolution into performance art, says she cannot keep track anymore. Ten days later Kiro posts a Daya exhibit captioned **`Daya showering in the holy divine light of raja:`** and Daya joins the wheeze reactions. The attachment itself remains undescribed. The social reversal is enough: the person trying to identify the defendant becomes an exhibit herself.

The communal-doodle material adds one more recognizable Daya move. She wants the finished project used as the Raja poster, floats the server banner too, and later asks for the final banana copy so she can use that on Raja in the meantime. That is not artist credit. It is Daya taking the group's private clown object and trying to put it on the official-facing wall. The same person who routes humans through the house also routes house-made nonsense outward.

The Recruiter title is therefore only the cleanest label for one part of the story. The fuller progression is recovered checkpoint → Officer promotion → real-life-first advice → practical onboarding → safety maintenance → music kept → established house person who can both operate the door and get dragged into Screenshot Jail with the rest of the clowns.

## LATER RECORD

The person-specific authored record gets thinner after the scene-rich 2022–2023 stretch. Current Pit Boss / Minister status and Recruiter are confirmed canon, but the archive does not safely give an appointment date for every later title. Fewer recovered public scenes are not proof that Daya vanished, hardened, or became more formal. Voice chat, DMs, game chat, and ordinary private social life are not fully preserved in this dossier.

What the surviving scenes do make clear is that Daya's authority never needs to become her entire public personality. Her most characteristic receipts are often very small: a heart after a welcome, a useful room while somebody waits, permission not to repeat the rules, a song migrating into Spotify, a cursed poster being promoted to official surface, a pair of sunglasses after the Wall has already won.
