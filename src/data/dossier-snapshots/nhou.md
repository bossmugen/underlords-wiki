# Nhou

Nhou is current **Staff**, and the first thing the wiki has to remove from his old characterization is a fake department. The archive does **not** earn `UL medical consultant`. Mugen once routes a cursed medical-adjacent question toward him, yes, but the actual scene is an ensemble joke in which everybody conspires to make the answer worse. Nhou's better recurring shape is smaller: he often answers a situation with one compact sideways line and lets the room finish detonating around it.

That compression is already visible in April 2020. Nhou announces **`i got the last thing`**. Fifteen seconds later: **`i dont remember what it was lol`**. The archive does not recover the object cleanly enough to name it, and naming it would not improve the scene anyway. Success report, immediate loss of the success's noun. Done.

September gives the signature exhibit. Mugen asks: **`@nhou how do we explain coffee enemas`**. Illien says they seem self-explanatory. Anthos asks whether the bags are full of coffee, produces `0_0`, goes to `goodle`, corrects herself to `google`; Anayss contributes `o.o`. At the point a normal person might attempt to restore medical clarity, Nhou posts:

> `U can have latte or frapp`

Mugen immediately adds **`extra foam too`**. Nhou reacts with laughter. Later Illien says she will still be taking her coffee the regular way. This is not a consultation. It is the entire room declining the opportunity to become more responsible.

Six days later Anthos throws `u mean a coffee enema` back toward Nhou, which is enough to show the joke remained socially attached to him for at least a moment. The wiki still does not need a second coffee scene. Recurrence is useful; repetition for repetition's sake is not.

On September 24 Nhou posts **`i just wokeup`** and later the export preserves at least seven rapid single-letter `z` messages. It is an excellent dated bit and a terrible foundation for diagnosing an always-sleepy personality. A week later, as Club Only empties after goodnights, Nhou writes **`everyone gone just like that`** and **`gnightt`**. The archive file gets better when it lets both exist: the absurd z sequence and the very ordinary awareness that the room has gone quiet.

Photo Submissions gives him a tiny self-caption with unusually visible reception. On July 15, 2021, Nhou posts a PNG under **`Nhouwu + duck`**. The post gets seven `Raja_dino_heart` reactions; Gabu, Ansun, and Ren are among the safely resolved named reactors. The image itself was not visually inspected, so `Nhouwu + duck` stays exactly what it is: Nhou's caption. It does not prove who took the image, who made it, who appears in it, or whether Nhou owns any duck involved in the situation.

The Wall later catches the same economy doing a different job. In September 2021 the room is talking about Suzimasu as one of UL's founders and his relationship to afterhours nonsense. Nhou writes **`Suzi freaked out since the beginning xD`**. Five minutes later Mugen uses a true Discord Reply to Nhou's exact message and answers **`Yus`**. That is a cleaner later-year receipt than inventing a personality from reaction counts: one compact house-memory characterization, directly affirmed by the person who had already supplied the founder context.

The boundary matters. `Since the beginning` does not prove Nhou personally stood at UL's founding moment or witnessed every early Suzi incident. Mugen's surrounding text establishes the founder context; Nhou supplies the character summary; Mugen confirms that summary. The wiki keeps those jobs separate instead of turning three messages into eyewitness testimony.

There are more Nhou traces — unresolved Wall attachments, a `stripper's knowledge` tag from Anayss, a 2022 Lobby self-introduction with real-world biographical details — but none improves the public file enough to justify exposing or guessing. His biography is already coherent without padding. He gets the last thing and forgets the thing. He is asked for an explanation and offers a frapp. He wakes up and becomes z. He sees the room empty and says goodnight. He posts `Nhouwu + duck`. He drops one line about Suzi and Mugen says yes.

That is a person file, not a medical department.
