# Milo

Milo could wander into Whiskey with an ordinary update about class, working out, a lab interruption, or drinking so much water that an AFK needed explaining. Winter coffee had a clear winner: **peppermint mocha**. Yaza could turn Milo's request for beauty sleep into an insult with one cheerful **“Yeah you need it!!”**. Nobody needed to stage a grand entrance.

## Gilli Had Witnesses

Put Gilli in the conversation and Milo's soft status-update voice developed teeth. Their back-and-forth ranged from game-strength smack talk to a later invitation for a “kith,” answered with a head-kiss emote. They were comfortable enough to keep reopening the same joke.

On December 15, 2020, Gilli posted a Discord screenshot and tagged Milo. Milo's edited response was immediate in spirit: **“You mf ... haha thought no one seen that.”** He did not deny being caught. He objected to the existence of witnesses. The picture itself is uninspected, and the alleged offense remains gloriously unspecified.

## WAIT EOS HII BESTIE

A club merger suddenly put Eos in Milo's path on January 29, 2021. Milo burst out with **“WAIT EOS HII BESTIE”**, then marveled that they were in the same club. Eos had not known Milo was there either. A roster change became a reunion before anyone finished talking about the roster.

## The Slow Exit Plan

By April, Milo was tired enough of Dragon Raja's grind to propose going free-to-play and slowly quitting. Eos greeted the prospect of restraint with **“You finally limiting yourself, u whale ... good.”** Milo's **“shush we don’t gotta talk about it”** was not exactly a rebuttal. The record catches the plan, not proof that Milo followed through.

## Relationships

**Gilli** — A repeat sparring partner who could turn a screenshot into a public prosecution and pivot straight into exaggerated affection.

**Eos** — An already-familiar friend whose unexpected same-club arrival made Milo audibly happy.

**Yaza** — An easy Whiskey conversational partner, equally available for coffee agreement and beauty-sleep slander.
