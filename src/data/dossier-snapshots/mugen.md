# Mugen

Mugen founded Underlords in March 2020. Fine. Put that on the little brass plaque and get it out of the way, because the archive has spent six years preserving a much stranger and more useful person than **Founder / Boss**.

The woman underneath the title can scare somebody on first contact and later become the same person's goofy older-friend problem. She describes herself as a drifter people come to for perspective, while also being the dork who hypes people up and plans the trip. She is an RN/director whose body keeps losing arguments with sleep. She wants systems, asks other people when the system is stupid, likes the joke until the joke starts making somebody's life harder, and has apparently been saying some version of **please do not lose the shit** since 2020.

She has been LadyMugen, `bossmugen`, somebody's mum, somebody else's mama, an irresponsible Magic School Bus mother, a Plutonian dumdum, and — because she once failed to use a mute button correctly — real leather papa.

The Boss title is real. It just never manages to eat Mugs.

## Before Boss, there was Mugs

UL did not invent Mugen's tendency to turn online relationships into long-lived family language. Before Dragon Raja there was another game and another circle: Dean, Key, Zepp, Kal, New, GenGrey. Dean was already her online son and already called her `mum`.

Later UL material treats that bond as inherited reality. When Anthos is working on the Tumbleweed and writes `lets add dean to mugs then from there we can add cel and woo etc`, the room is not inventing Dean's placement. It is trying to draw something that already existed.

The family map arrived after the family language.

So did Boss.

## LadyMugen gets told to do it herself

In Dragon Raja Server 10 she was **LadyMugen**. She was with Intent, leader of ScarletMoon. Mugen kept offering solutions to problems around some of his officers. Intent eventually challenged her to lead something herself.

Years later, when QOTD asks how UL started, Mugen tells essentially the same story from her own side: she kept giving personnel solutions, got challenged to run her own guild, happened to have a few hours off, and went, more or less, *why not*.

That is the useful beginning. Before the organization, there was already a woman who apparently could not watch a problem sit there without touching it.

Ritha, WolfPhenix, Suzimasu and HyaLuna came over from Altissa and became the rest of the five-person founding circle. They do not need to be flattened into a founder list. Ritha later becomes the strategic battle brain. Wolf keeps leaving art and screenshots behind. Suzi remains part of the old social fabric. HyaLuna is attached to some of UL's earliest surviving visual memory.

Mugen begins in the middle of people, not above them.

## Mugs according to Mugs

QOTD finally gives us something better than inferred personality: Mugen describing herself.

In June 2022 she calls herself a **`distant drifter friend`**. She is the person people come to for perspective or a gameplan. She also describes herself as a dork, the hype person, and the planner who organizes trips and events.

That combination explains more of the archive than Founder does.

She can be socially mobile without being detached. She may drift, but when somebody comes to her with a problem her instinct is to start arranging the pieces. And when the problem is not a problem at all, she may be the idiot trying to get everybody into the car.

A March 2022 love-language QOTD gives **Acts of Service + Quality Time**. It is a self-report, not a diagnosis, and it lines up suspiciously well with the woman the rest of the archive keeps catching: doing the task, making the room, checking the list, finding the person, planning the thing, and spending enough years around the same idiots that the relationship outlives the game.

Mugen's own description is useful because it is less grand than the role other people gave her.

Drifter. Perspective. Gameplan. Dork. Hype. Planner.

That sounds much more like Mugs.

## Apparently she was scary

She was.

QOTD first-impression threads preserve a remarkably consistent arc: Mugen reads as intimidating, adult, leader-like, somebody who can absolutely **lay down the hammer**. Then people know her longer and the description mutates toward kind, soft, goofy.

The second half does not cancel the first.

That is exactly why the archive works better when it does not sand Mugen into Generic Caring Internet Mom. The warmth lands because the sharper version is still in the room. People can know that she is capable of making a decision stick and also know that she may lose the next argument to a nap, a typo, a hot mic, or her own terrible display name.

Woosung gives one of the best views from the other side. Woo describes Mugen as a close friend, an older-sister-like guide, a grown-up version of herself, and somebody she can talk to about anything. That is an independent friendship, not romance residue: Woo is the female cousin of Mugen's ex.

So the social progression is not **scary Mugen was secretly fake**.

It is: scary Mugen is what you may meet first. Mugs is what becomes visible after you stay.

## Real Leather Papa: a microphone crime

The officers were in VC having an actual serious conversation.

Mugen failed to mute.

Somewhere in real life she was discussing a genuine cowhide whip from a Texas cowboy, and the room suddenly heard:

`real leather, papa`

That was enough. A title was born with no dignity whatsoever.

The phrase escaped the officer room, became a Staff joke, then a wider identity. By 2023 Mugen herself is talking about **`my real leather papa 2020 era making a comeback`** and yelling `LEADER WHO? I M JSUT A REAL LEATHER PAPA`.

Then Father's Day arrives and Ren wishes leather papa a happy Father's Day.

Mugen answers:

`Real leather papa day`

One of her longest-lived identities came from being overheard through the wrong microphone setting. Instead of killing the joke, she eventually starts writing sequels for it.

## The Boss is probably asleep right now

Employment remains one of Mugen's great anti-mythology devices.

She is an RN and works in healthcare leadership as a director. The social archive rarely treats that as a résumé line. Work enters as exhaustion, timing, missed nonsense, after-work collapse, somebody asking where Mugen is and somebody else knowing the answer is probably horizontal.

As early as April 2020, Gilli tells a newcomer Mugen is `probably asleep right now`. In 2021 Mugen posts an old photo captioned `Old pic, lying ded on floor after work`. In 2023: `I take a short nap and wake up to this`.

The reunion gives us a better work line because it comes from Mugen herself. During nursing talk with Kiro she calls herself **`that senior nurse`** and then summarizes the experience behind that seniority as:

`11 years of mistakes bb`

There she is.

Competence, seniority, and immediate sabotage of the pedestal.

The conversation can move between actual nursing experience and an IV-pole stripclub joke without either register needing to defeat the other. Mugen has a serious real-world profession. She also refuses to narrate herself like a LinkedIn page.

UL learned the more useful version anyway.

Boss is asleep. Continue the emergency.

## Control freak, not oracle

Mugen likes systems.

Rooms, processes, events, visual identities, role structures, archives, lists, recurring things that become named things: if a social need keeps happening, there is a decent chance she will eventually try to give it a container.

From far away that can look like somebody who needs control.

Up close, the archive keeps catching her asking for help.

`accurate or no, thoughts?`

Officer rooms and event work preserve the same habit: missing information, corrections, opinions, verification, people with specialties being asked to use them. During the 2026 reunion, Mugen can remember enough people to operate as a retrieval engine and still need Snow and Sye to remind her how to assign Discord roles.

That is not a flaw in the biography. It is the good part.

She likes knowing what the fuck is going on. She does not appear to require pretending that she always does.

## Chaos gets a railing

Mugen likes the bit.

She also repeatedly notices when the bit starts charging somebody else rent.

The early archive gives the hard-line version in casual syntax: `everyone is impulsive but minors are a no fly zone pls`. Slice also catches her objecting when somebody saves another person's photo without permission.

Then 2023 April Fools Month gives a more ridiculous example. The server spends weeks mutating names until people are genuinely struggling to identify who anybody is. Mugen participates in the prank season. She also supports accommodations once recognizability becomes an actual access problem for people trying to socialize.

That is a recurring Mugen shape worth keeping precise.

Not **fun police**. Not **anything goes**.

Make the joke. Keep the joke. Install a railing when somebody starts falling off the fucking platform.

A boundary does not need formal language to be real. A stupid environment does not need to stop being stupid just because the boundary exists.

## The irresponsible Magic School Bus mother

Mugen does not merely get called mum.

She participates.

Daycare gives the cleanest version. In the room built around Platelet / younger-member social grammar, Mugen self-casts as **`the irresponsible mom who takes them out on a magic schoolbus`**.

That is considerably more specific than Community Mother.

She is the parent who arrives, watches the kids, and apparently considers educational field trips acceptable only if liability has been abandoned.

Years later an older participant points out they are not a Platelet. Mugen's answer is simple:

`You're still my kid`

Meanwhile she is perfectly capable of telling the oldies to leave the playground, catching herself mid-conversation with `oh wait daycare`, confirming that yes, everyone was trying to break another bot, and proposing the plot twist that **`the daycare is a front for the UL morgue`** where the corpses get dumped after use.

This is how Mugen does maternal language in UL.

Food, supervision, permanence, family vocabulary — and then a concealed morgue under the playpen.

Dean calls her `mum`. Akamin calls her `mama`. Sye participates in family-role nonsense with her. Tumbleweed tries to diagram the resulting disaster.

The caring part is real.

So is the Magic School Bus negligence.

## She tries one clean emotion and everybody panics

Mugen is sentimental. The archive has known this for years.

Thanksgiving 2022 gets a long gratitude message thanking Staff, Strippers, Bartenders, Bouncers, Platelets, Tumbleweed family, VIPs and Officers, including work that often goes unseen.

She signs it:

`Your resident plutonian dumdum.`

In May 2023 she tells people `This is your house` and `The circus tent is always open`, then closes with `Bless you my children`, immediately adds `Whoever you are.`, and signs `xoxo real leather papa`.

The joke usually arrives before the sincerity can sit alone too long.

Then the 2026 reunion gives us the experiment where she does **not** immediately vandalize the feeling.

Mugen says:

`It's been a while.`

Apparently this is so suspicious that the room reacts with alarm.

Mugen finally yells:

`OK WHY ARE YALL SCARED I WAS TRYING TO BE SENTIMENTAL`

That may be the cleanest receipt we have for her emotional style because the room itself supplies the control group.

Mugen being sentimental is normal.

Mugen being sentimental **without a joke attached** is apparently cause for concern.

## Don't lose the shit

The current archive project did not invent Mugen's preservation problem.

The first surviving sentence in the PR workroom, December 2020, is Mugen asking people to dump promotional material there:

`so we dont lose them`

In 2021 she posts Souta's work in the gallery:

`for posterity uwu`

She reposts old material and keeps creator credits attached. HyaLuna remains the credited photo source on an early poster. Gilli remains the editor. Other people's work is allowed to stay theirs even when Mugen is the person carrying it forward.

By 2026 the scale is absurdly larger: exports, screenshots, old names, identity bridges, returning people, years of channels, the history short film, this entire archive-mining project.

But the instinct is old.

Do not lose the poster.

Do not lose who made the poster.

Eventually: do not let the archive lose the person because it happened to remember the username badly.

## Menace with glitter on it

Mugen has taste, and the taste is not clean prestige.

She says she dressed **Decora** in high school. Dragon Raja gives us **LadyMugen**, an older self-presentation that can later become cringe material once enough years pass. Photo Submissions gives her official **Mafia** and **Popstar** personas. UL iconography leans into neon, kisses, `xoxo`, animated affection, camp figures blowing kisses, mafia language, and **neighborhood-friendly mafia**.

It is menace with glitter on it.

Boss, but make it a nightclub flyer. Mafia, but everybody is blowing kisses. Affection, but sign it from Pluto. Self-mythology, but leave enough stupidity in the frame that nobody can mistake it for worship.

She seems to enjoy making a version of herself and then surviving long enough to be embarrassed by it.

That matters.

A person who self-mythologizes but cannot tolerate later laughter becomes unbearable very quickly.

Mugen keeps leaving the embarrassing old versions where her friends can find them.

## The people do not belong to the game anymore

Mugen's long memory is less interesting as a list of names than as a behavior.

Aeshleen says Raja is over for her; Mugen tells her she will `always be part of the UL fam`.

Em says redownloading Raja is too much and thanks people for remembering her while contributing to a communal drawing. Mugen answers:

`Emmm even without raja you're still part of the fam!`

The game keeps trying to end relationships that the room has already moved somewhere else.

Mugen does not seem especially interested in granting the game custody.

The same instinct gets louder in 2026 when people start actually returning. She tags old names. Xephy answers after six years. Mugen posts the return DMs. The room starts digging for more people.

`WHO ELSE WE DRAGGIN BACK TO THE CIRCUS TENT`

This is not passive nostalgia. It is retrieval.

And retrieval is messy. Mugen remembers some people instantly and needs help with other details. Old roles blur. Names changed. Somebody remembers the image but not the date. Somebody else knows exactly who made the thing. The reconstruction works because memory is distributed across the house.

Mugen does not need to be the perfect memory bank to be one of the people most aggressively ringing the dinner bell.

## Don't burn the house down, I have a meeting

The 2026 reunion produces one of the best collisions between Boss, Mugs and ordinary adult life.

Mugen has been helping reanimate a server, finding people, checking on people, making old references boot again, and generally behaving like somebody discovered necromancy in the Discord settings.

Then she has to leave for an actual meeting.

Her instruction to the room:

`Don't burn the house down`

She tags Wolf, Gilli and Gabu:

`babysit halp`

The replies are not reassuring.

This is Mugen's whole problem in miniature. She can be central without believing the room ceases to exist when she leaves. She can hand the house sideways. She can go do her real job while everybody else continues whatever regrettable experiment was already underway.

Leadership is part of the scene.

The much better part is that her calendar does not care that a six-year digital séance is in progress.

## Mugs

Strip away the Boss résumé and a fairly coherent woman is still standing there.

She can be intimidating on first contact and ridiculous once she trusts the room enough to stop protecting the image. She describes herself as a drifter people come to for perspective, a hype person, a planner. She says she values Acts of Service and Quality Time. She likes systems, asks for corrections, and can admit she does not remember how the system works anymore. She draws hard lines around things she considers real while allowing almost everything around those lines to remain stupid.

She works too much. She naps. She has eleven years of mistakes, bb. She has taste that keeps wandering toward mafia glamour, neon, kisses, camp and visual self-invention. She has a preservation reflex old enough to predate the archive project by years. She can become mum, mama and papa without resolving any of those titles into a sane family structure. She will call herself the irresponsible Magic School Bus mother and then tell an adult they are still her kid.

She is sentimental. She usually damages the sentiment herself before anybody else gets the chance. The one time she tries a clean `It's been a while`, everybody gets scared.

She remembers people for a long time. When she cannot remember enough, she asks somebody else. When the old people answer, she starts looking for more.

Most importantly, leadership never required her to become less available as a person.

UL was allowed to know her well enough to tease her, correct her, wake her, remember her old outfits, steal her failed mute into a title, watch her professional life leak into Discord, and become suspicious when she attempts one unaccompanied emotion.

Mugen built the house, yes.

Then she lived in it long enough that everybody knows the Boss is probably asleep — and that if she wakes up sentimental, something is deeply wrong.
