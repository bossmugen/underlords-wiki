# Kiro

Kiro — also Gum and, on the same stable account, HicUUOOOOGH — is a retired full Officer and historical Pit Boss / Minister. The cleaner character story is not "Officer who did Officer things." The strongest surviving material keeps catching a much more specific social rhythm: Kiro is recognized, he resists the recognition just long enough to turn it into a joke, and then he often makes the joke harder to resolve than it was before he arrived.

The record is still thin on his pre-UL life, hobbies, tastes, and exact first entrance. That gap stays a gap. What survives well is his house voice: short replies, low ceremony, easy baiting, and an ability to move from ordinary real-life talk into UL nonsense without needing a separate performance register.

## ROLE PROGRESSION

### August–September 2022 — the bouncer says onboarding; Mugs says gummy bear

The first clean textual return in the current catalog is August 31, 2022. The Stripclub Bouncer gives Kiro the standard welcome/role-assignment machinery. Kiro identifies himself as **`『Gυмツ』`** in UnderLords. Mugen replies **`Welcome gummy bear!`**. Kiro answers **`Helloooo`**, and Mugen points him toward the Discord Tour, Whiskey, and Biodata.

Eight days later the machinery repeats. Mugen does not. She opens with **`Hey gummy bear! You know the drill`**. Kiro says he had to copy-paste the information because he lost it; Mugen finds what she needs and closes with **`welcome back gummieeees`**.

The record does not tell us why the account goes through role assignment twice in eight days, so the dossier does not manufacture a ban/rejoin saga. The useful point is simpler: Kiro is already being treated as a familiar returning person before the formal Officer announcement.

### October 2022 — Officer / Pit Boss

On October 1, Mugen publicly announces HicUUOOOOGH as UL's newest Officer and thanks him for his **`service`** and for **`looking after our family`**. That is the safe promotion date and the safest description of how Mugen evaluated him at that moment.

What the record does *not* give is a sufficiently complete ledger of the labor behind those words. The title is real; the missing résumé stays missing. His later retirement is canon, but the retirement date remains unresolved.

## RUNNING GAG — PETTY PROSECUTOR, CARTOON DEFENDANT

Kiro knows the Wall from both sides of the desk.

On October 8, 2022 he labels his own filing **`Being petty`**. Gilli true-replies **`DID YOU GET IT? :Cat_Sporkle:`**. Kiro true-replies with the entire mission report: **`No:EmoJi_CrYiNg:`**. The reply chain is exact. Whatever **it** was is not; the screenshot stays sealed.

Six days later Kiro files again: **`Seraph needs some explaining to do:Cute_catskrunky:`**. Two self-initiated receipt posts in six days are enough to make the prosecution instinct recurrent rather than accidental. He does not pretend neutrality. He is willing to announce that the filing is petty and proceed anyway.

The defendant version of Kiro is much less interested in explanation. Daya says **`@HicUUOOOOGH im callin the FDA`**; Kiro reacts with a crying cat and later posts a SpongeBob run-away GIF in the same local pocket. Another summons eventually gets three `:z_bang:` emotes. In April 2023 Daya correctly asks whether Hic is Kiro; Kiro gives **`N-n-n-no..`**, Momo answers **`Yeppers`**, and Kiro immediately escalates to **`Who's kiro`**.

That is the usable contradiction: when Kiro brings the case, he names it. When the case is Kiro, suddenly the defense strategy is crying, fleeing, gunfire emotes, or abolishing the defendant's identity. The Wall does not catch two personalities. It catches one person who apparently enjoys both roles.

### Relationship — Gilli

Gilli is one of the clearest recurring counterparts in Kiro's receipt game. Her October 8 follow-up is a direct Reply to Kiro's own filing, and Kiro's `No` is a direct Reply back. Later she can post **`Rip Kiro, he will be missed.`**, tag him, and Kiro probably answers the local bit with a snow-dive GIF instead of stopping to ask what the premise is supposed to be.

The later join is contextual rather than structural, so the dossier keeps it probable. The social read does not need inflation: Gilli and Kiro repeatedly understand the Wall as something they can do *with* each other, whether that means checking whether the petty operation succeeded or converting Kiro himself into the exhibit.

## IDENTITY PLAY — WHO'S KIRO

The April 2023 scene works because everybody involved already knows the answer. Daya asks, **`LMAO is @HicUUOOOOGH kiro`**. Kiro answers **`N-n-n-no..`**. Momo says **`Yeppers`**. Kiro immediately follows with **`Who's kiro`**. Daya gives up: **`i cant keep track anymore 🤣😭`**.

The identity is not actually unresolved. The stable account bridge is solid. The joke is Kiro watching the filing system successfully resolve him and then kicking the cabinet over. Daya's exasperated line gets crying reactions from both Momo and Kiro, so he is visibly laughing inside the confusion he helped create.

## 2026 — ADULT LIFE SHOWS UP; THE HOUSE REGISTER DOES NOT LEAVE

The reunion catches Kiro years later in a much more adult conversation without turning him into a completely different character. Mugen greets him in the old register and asks what he has been doing. Kiro answers **`still hoeing around :3`** before the room moves into real nursing/work talk.

That professional layer matters because it gives the dossier ordinary-life texture beyond Discord roles. Kiro and Mugen can talk in shared nursing/work vocabulary, including administrative frustration and paperwork, then fold the whole thing back into UL's stripclub language and IV-pole jokes almost immediately. Serious domain fluency and ridiculous social fluency sit next to each other without announcement.

The real-world boundary stays narrow. The conversation supports familiarity with nursing/work settings and Kiro speaking from inside that vocabulary. It does not establish an exact current title, employer, unit, school, location, or credentials.

## WHAT STAYS RECOGNIZABLY KIRO

The strongest cross-year pattern is a social motion. Kiro gets recognized as a returning house person before the Officer title. On the Wall he can prosecute petty nonsense and then turn himself into a cartoon defendant when prosecution comes back around. Once the room resolves `HicUUOOOOGH` back to Kiro, he asks **`Who's kiro`**. Years later, ordinary work talk can still be punctured by **`still hoeing around :3`** and UL's old stripclub grammar.

He does not need to be "always evasive" or "always unserious" for the pattern to hold. The 2026 material catches enough concrete real-life talk to show that he can be specific when the subject needs it. The contradiction is the interesting part: adult professional, former Officer, petty filer, theatrical defendant. None of those apparently required him to stop being Kiro.
