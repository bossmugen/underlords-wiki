# Nuien

Nuien is current **Staff**, historically visible in the recovered 2020 Discord account as **Nui**, and attached in house language to **`papi`**. The archive finally gives that tag enough context to stop it floating around like a random hover-card joke, while also giving the wiki a crucial legal notice in twelve-foot letters: **Nuien is not Illien**.

The first useful scene is May 18, 2020. Nui writes **`Text mehh about cc heheh`**, then turns the room into a live inventory counter: **`Hurry hurry TWO SPOTS KEFT`**, then **`ONE SPOT HURRYYYY`**, then **`SOLD OUT`**. A Deleted User answers one of the calls, but that account remains pooled and unresolved. More importantly, `cc` itself is not safely expanded by the source. The wiki therefore keeps the acronym and the behavior: whatever the call was for, Nuien moved from open invitation to scarcity to closure in real time.

That does not appoint Nuien Recruiter, Event Director, booking agent, or Head of Two Spots Left. It shows operational urgency with a live room. The difference matters because the scene is specific enough without needing a badge.

September 4 gives a quieter continuity clue. A fresh Lobby bot card appears for Nui. Gilli follows with role routing and **`welcome home`**. The human wording matters more than the generic bot state: `welcome home` reads like recognition of somebody returning, not like strangers establishing first contact. The archive still does not tell us why the account was being re-carded, so September 4 stays a re-entry scene rather than a manufactured membership-origin date.

Nine days later Nuien and Anthos stop using language responsibly. Nui posts **`TOIT NUPS`**. Anthos repeats **`TOIT NUPS`**. Nui upgrades the typography to **`***TOIT NUPS***`**. Anthos attempts to formalize the outbreak into call-and-response: **`WHEN I SAY TOIT U SAY`**. Zoshaa enters as the only available public representative of reality and asks **`ᵗᶠ ⁱˢ ᵗᵒⁱᵗ ⁿᵘᵖˢ`**. Anthos says `TOIT`; Nui says `NUPS`; the loop continues. Nui later posts a Tenor link for the Brooklyn Nine-Nine `nups / toit` bit.

That is a genuine two-person rhythm rather than mere co-presence. Anthos is actively repeating, setting up the response structure, and handing the next syllable back to Nuien. Zoshaa's confusion makes the shared grammar visible because suddenly somebody in the room admits there is apparently a grammar to be confused by. None of that requires a friendship ranking or special-pair mythology. It is enough to say: on this date, Anthos and Nuien can sustain the same stupid premise together for longer than the room's comprehension survives.

September 29 is where `papi` finally gets a social address. Ren's pinned **Underlords Organisation Update** — one of the fake family/relationship charts, not governance — places **`Nuien (papi)`** under one branch. The same artifact separately lists **`ILLIEN - alcoholic noodle`**, which is period evidence that the two identities were distinct even inside the contemporary joke map.

Then a later chart update leaves Nuien out. Ansun notices almost immediately: **`WHAT HAPPEN TO NUIEN`**, followed by **`ISNT NUI THE MOM AND YOUR THE DAD`**, directed toward Anthos. Anthos answers **`YESSSS`**. The literal family language is nonsense, but the recognition is real. Nuien disappears from the joke structure once and somebody opens an audit.

That is the strongest current Nuien scene because it does three jobs without overreaching. It grounds `papi` in actual period house language. It proves Nuien and Illien were being tracked separately. And it shows Nuien was socially established enough inside the bit that omission itself looked wrong to Ansun.

Other fragments remain deliberately backstage. In May, Nuien threatens **`my ass will QUIT QUIT`**; Ren answers `NU NU`; Gilli says **`No you WONT WONT`**; Nuien immediately folds with **`OKAY I WONT WONT`**. It is a beautiful ensemble bounce, but the trigger is not reconstructed tightly enough yet to make it a public chapter. Likewise, Mugen's early **`GET IN THE WHISKEY ROOM BIIIIIHHHH`** after Nui's `BITCHH` shows chemistry and routing, not a special relationship tier.

So the current Nuien file stays lean and specific. A shrinking-spots countdown. A `welcome home`. A `TOIT NUPS` outbreak shared with Anthos. A fake-family chart where leaving `papi` off once causes immediate clerical panic. That is enough to make Nuien a person rather than a tag stack without manufacturing the missing later-era arc.
