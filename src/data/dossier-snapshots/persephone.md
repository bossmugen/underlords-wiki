# Persephone

Persephone arrived at the Lobby with a heart attached to the name: **`Persephone<3 or EmmaXoxo`**. She named Underlords as her club and, twelve days later, appeared in the FLOWERS Photoshoot submissions. She did not need a running commentary to keep participating.

By September, she was back for **FESTIVAL**. The image went up first. Seventeen seconds later came the little afterthought: **“my submission ^^ sorry if it’s not perfect lol”**. The apology did not stop her from pressing send. That is the lovely small contradiction here: the work was already in the room before she started negotiating with its imagined imperfections. Her FLOWERS post had drawn ten `Ds_NAENAE` reactions; FESTIVAL got eight hearts. She wasn't just dropping pictures into a void. People were there, reacting, while she worried about whether the second one was good enough.

Five years later, Mugs called **“persephone home”** into the August reunion. Persephone came back, said hello, confirmed she'd had breakfast, and then admitted she was hungry again. After all those old Photoshoot posts, her grand return was not a speech. It was the very ordinary business of being remembered, answering, and immediately needing food.
