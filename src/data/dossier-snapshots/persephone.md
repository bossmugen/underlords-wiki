# Persephone

Persephone arrived at the Lobby with a heart attached to the name: **`Persephone<3 or EmmaXoxo`**. She named Underlords as her club and, twelve days later, appeared in the FLOWERS Photoshoot submissions. She did not need a running commentary to keep participating.

By September, she was back for **FESTIVAL**. The image went up first. Seventeen seconds later came the little afterthought: **“my submission ^^ sorry if it’s not perfect lol”**. The apology did not stop her from pressing send. That is the lovely small contradiction here: the work was already in the room before she started negotiating with its imagined imperfections.
