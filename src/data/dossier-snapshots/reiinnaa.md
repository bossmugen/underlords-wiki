# Reiinnaa

Reiinnaa came through UL's Lobby as a visitor from Forsaken, made it into the Photoshoot ledger almost immediately, and returned later that month with a replacement Discord account. The memorable part is how little ceremony the return required: she explained that the old account had died, and Gilli went straight to the practical question of whether to kick the abandoned one. A person could lose the login without having to introduce herself from scratch.
