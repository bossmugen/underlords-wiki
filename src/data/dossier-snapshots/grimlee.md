# Grimlee

Grimlee kept coming back. The Waltz round caught him in July, Dusk til Dawn followed, then Popstar, and by December he was still there for Winter. The posts themselves did not come with little speeches explaining his vision. He put the entries in the room and let them stand.

Four appearances across changing themes make a better introduction than pretending one blank caption tells us what kind of person he was. Grimlee was a repeat participant, not a stray name on a single contest roster. The person behind `father.grim` had enough interest in the game of dressing up a prompt to keep returning months later.
