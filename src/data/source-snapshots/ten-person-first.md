## Person-first synthesis allowed by the evidence

The repeated 2020 pattern is **low-friction social uptake**: Ten often accepts an available expressive grammar and answers inside it. Bark → animal-noise response; `owO` → `owo;`; morning → `mornin`; Wall reputation → legacy/heir; mock danger → stage-direction hammer and then `owo`; house taxonomy → apprentice / anomaly / joke adoption; bot affection → mirrored bot affection.

This is a recurring period-specific social pattern, not a universal psychological diagnosis. Do not turn it into claims about Ten's offline temperament where the archive is silent.
