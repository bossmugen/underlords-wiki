## Person-level synthesis allowed by these receipts

The current Ryo archive is concentrated in spring 2023, so the public biography should not pretend it captures a whole life. Inside that window, however, a recognizable social style does emerge. Ryo's reactions are often highly visible and compressed: all-caps shock, short mock-legal amendments, a sentence collapsing into one letter and then cat emotes, or a quick `Loser` after somebody explains why they used a crying reaction. The archive also catches a practical counter-motion: alarm can become `I got this`, `I can fix this`, or an explicit apology very quickly.

The safest formulation is therefore scene-bound rather than psychological. In these recovered interactions, Ryo tends to let the reaction be seen, then often re-enters the situation rather than staying stuck in the reaction. The same person who performs reputational death over a Wall starring can shift to repair language when something actually needs fixing, and can answer UL's grand soul-theft claim with the extremely small `Not yet.`

Moon is the strongest relationship thread because Ryo explicitly names Moon as the invitation route and Moon publicly celebrates the first Wall starring. That supports an existing friendship / social connection, but not a start date, exclusivity claim, or closeness ranking.
